### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the mandatory protocols for Google Business Profile optimization, local transactional keyword targeting, automated review generation, and digital-to-offline lead routing to authorized dealer counters across Rajasthan.

---

### 2. Google Business Profile (GBP) Management Standards
Every primary and satellite listing must strictly adhere to the following setup:
1. **Naming Standard**: `Swatch Paints — Factory & Experience Center, [City Name]`
2. **Category Architecture**: Primary: `Paint Manufacturer`. Secondary: `Paint Store`, `Coatings Supplier`, `Building Materials Store`.
3. **Hours & Responsiveness**: 08:00 AM – 08:00 PM IST Monday through Saturday. Automated WhatsApp messaging active 24/7.
4. **Geotagged Photo Protocol**: Minimum 5 photographs per week uploaded with embedded metadata (GPS coordinates, image title containing target city and keyword).
5. **Review Target**: Minimum 5 new 5-star reviews per week, each containing specific keywords (e.g., *Rustic texture, Bundi paint, exterior coating*).

---

### 3. Digital Lead Routing Protocol (SLA: 15 Minutes)
Whenever an inbound call, WhatsApp message, or Google Maps inquiry is received:
- **Minute 0–2**: Automated AI acknowledgment via WhatsApp Gateway greeting the customer and inquiring on their project city and estimated square footage.
- **Minute 2–5**: Lead categorization into Tier 1 (Commercial/Builder $\ge 100$ bags), Tier 2 (Homeowner 20–50 bags), or Tier 3 (Retail single room $< 20$ bags).
- **Minute 5–10**: Automated assignment to the nearest authorized dealer based on geographic proximity.
- **Minute 10–15**: Notification dispatched to authorized dealer with customer name, phone number, and project scope; simultaneous SMS sent to customer with dealer's address and Google Maps directions link.

### 4. Review Generation & Thekedar Advocacy Protocol
- **Trigger**: Upon confirmation of a 20-bag or 50-bag purchase by a contractor:
  1. Hermes WhatsApp Gateway sends automated gratitude message with a 1-click Google review link.
  2. Incentive: Submitting a verified review with an attached photo of the finished wall enters the thekedar into the monthly ₹5,000 contractor lucky draw.
  3. Every review must be officially acknowledged and replied to within 24 hours.
