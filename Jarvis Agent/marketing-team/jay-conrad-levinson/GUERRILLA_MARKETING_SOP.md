### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the mandatory operational protocols for executing zero-to-low-budget guerrilla street activations, 48-hour commercial blitzkriegs, live roadside demonstrations, contractor gamification showdowns, and viral word-of-mouth initiatives across Rajasthan.

---

### 2. The 48-Hour Saturation Blitzkrieg Protocol
When entering a new sub-market or town corridor, field teams must execute the standardized 48-hour blitz:
- **Phase 1 (Retail Anchor Mounting — Hours 0–12)**:
  - Install exterior fascia boards on minimum 5 primary mixed building material shops.
  - Erect 10-bag stacked entrance pyramids with high-gloss header cards.
  - Affix 50 durable counter mats and acrylic cash-drawer stickers.
- **Phase 2 (Roadside Live Demonstrations — Hours 12–24)**:
  - Paint minimum 1 prominent roadside half-and-half comparison wall with explicit owner consent.
  - Apply Swatch Rustic natural quartz texture on one half; contrast with peeling ordinary paint on the other.
- **Phase 3 (Contractor Tea Activation — Hours 24–48)**:
  - Host a "Chai & Charcha" session at the primary market tea stall for 15–20 local thekedars.
  - Execute the live trowel scratch challenge and distribute 15 sample paddles.

### 3. The Swatch Champion Craftsman Competition Protocol
- **Monthly Cadence**: Held on the last Saturday of every month in each district.
- **Rules of Engagement**:
  - Contractors register by submitting photos of completed residential facades applied with Swatch.
  - A panel consisting of Senior Chemist Shahrukh bhai and local field supervisors evaluates finish uniformity, trowel precision, and client satisfaction.
  - Winner receives the **Gold-Engraved Stainless Steel Venetian Trowel** + ₹2,500 cash voucher.
  - Winner's photo and quote are packaged into WhatsApp reels and local dealer counter cards.

---

### 4. Safety & Legal Compliance Standards
1. **Property Owner Consent**: 100% of physical walls, demo panels, and shopfronts must have verified verbal or written permission from the property owner. Vandalism is strictly prohibited.
2. **Public Space Respect**: Demonstrations must never block pedestrian walkways, obstruct traffic, or create public nuisance.
3. **Safety Gear**: Applicators executing live demos must wear clean, branded Swatch aprons and safety eyewear.
