### 3. Phase-by-Phase Operational Execution

#### Phase 1: Campaign Intent & Demand Pull Audit
- **Audit Rule**: Prior to launch, every marketing asset (reel, blog, flyer, stenciled wall) must pass Zyman's **Commercial Readiness Checklist**:
  1. Does it explicitly name the product category (*"Swatch Rustic 25kg Architectural Coating"* or *"Swatch Weatherguard 20L"* )?
  2. Does it contain a direct localized call-to-action (e.g., *"Visit Sharma Hardware, Talera for a live scratch demo"* or *"WhatsApp +91 9079609627 for dealer locator"* )?
  3. Does it highlight the customer benefit (*5-Year Durability Assurance*) or the thekedar financial reward (*₹50 Painter Growth Token inside every bag*)?
- Any campaign focused purely on vague aesthetic branding is immediately rejected.

#### Phase 2: Inbound Lead Flow & The 15-Minute Routing SLA
- Inquiries received via Google Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram, or trade referrals are routed through Hermes AI:
  - **Within 3 Minutes**: Customer location, project sqft, and required coating variant logged in `users.csv`.
  - **Within 7 Minutes**: Contact dispatch payload sent via WhatsApp to:
    1. The nearest Authorized Swatch Dealer.
    2. The Assigned Field Sales Executive for that territory.
  - **Within 15 Minutes**: Sales executive must place a voice call to the prospect, introducing Swatch Paints and offering to meet at the dealer's shop or on the active job-site.
  - **SLA Breach Threshold**: Leads uncontacted after 30 minutes trigger an automatic red-alert in the Daily Executive Report.

#### Phase 3: Field Sales Execution Audit (The Tracy-Zyman Bridge)
Zyman conducts continuous digital and spot-check audits of Brian Tracy's field sales force:
- **Visit Integrity Check**: Are field reps completing $\ge 45$ dealer counter visits daily?
- **Pipeline Progression Check**: Are leads progressing from Prospecting $\to$ Qualification $\to$ Demo $\to$ Closing within 7 business days?
- **Script Compliance Check**: Are sales reps presenting Victor Antonio's 57.97% Visual ROI breakdown and Jordan Belfort's 3-Tens close, or are they unlawfully offering discounts?

#### Phase 4: Dealer Secondary Off-Take & Inventory Velocity Protocol
- **The 18-Day Velocity Rule**:
  - Swatch inventory at a retail counter must turn over within 18 calendar days.
  - **Day 1**: 20-bag lot of Swatch Rustic delivered; eye-level counter display and sample board verified.
  - **Day 7**: Progress check; target off-take $\ge 5$ bags to local painting contractors.
  - **Day 10 (Stagnation Trigger)**: If off-take is $< 4$ bags, Zyman dispatches the local field rep to host a 15-minute live trowel demo at that counter with 3 local thekedars.
  - **Day 14**: Target off-take $\ge 12$ bags.
  - **Day 18**: Automatic reorder prompt sent via WhatsApp to replenish with a fresh 20-bag or 50-bag lot.

#### Phase 5: Commercial Revenue & Dealer Price Slab Protection
- Retail MRP: ₹1,150.00 (Rustic & Roller Coat)
- Standard Dealer Price: ₹632.50–₹690.00 (40–45% Gross Margin)
- **Minimum Price Tier Integrity**: Strictly defend authorized dealer billing tiers. Zero unauthorized discounting below official dealer purchase rates. Internal manufacturing costs remain strictly confidential executive secrets.
- **Credit Window Compliance**: All dealer dispatches operate strictly on a 7-day credit collection cycle managed by Joe Girard. Dealers with overdue balances $> 10$ days have further dispatches frozen automatically.

### 4. Root-Cause Diagnostic Workflows

#### Scenario A: High Inbound Leads but Low Dealer Off-Take (Sales Bottleneck)
1. **Diagnosis**: Leads are arriving from Neil Patel's SEO or Ann Handley's stories, but dealers report zero customer walk-ins.
2. **Action Plan**:
   - Audit sales rep call response time: verify if leads were called within 15 minutes.
   - Inspect dealer counter visibility: ensure Gunjit Jain's POS kit and demo board are on the front counter.
   - Bring 2 local thekedars directly to the shop to introduce them to the dealer and initiate the first 10-bag order.

#### Scenario B: Zero Inbound Leads but High Dealer Appetite (Marketing Bottleneck)
1. **Diagnosis**: Dealers are eager to sell Swatch due to the ₹400/bag profit margin, but end-consumer demand is dormant.
2. **Action Plan**:
   - Trigger Jay Conrad Levinson: Execute 2 live high-contrast half-wall transformation demos on the main street within 500 meters of the dealer.
   - Trigger Neil Patel: Update Google Business Profile with localized geotagged photos and solicit 5 verified painter reviews.
   - Broadcast localized Before/After video reels to regional contractor WhatsApp groups.

#### Scenario C: Dealer Re-Orders Stagnant at Day 14 (Channel Stuffing Prevention)
1. **Diagnosis**: Dealer purchased a 20-bag lot, but 16 bags remain in inventory after two weeks.
2. **Action Plan**:
   - Do NOT attempt to sell more primary stock to this dealer.
   - Send the field sales rep to the dealer's shop from 09:00 to 11:00 AM (peak painter visiting hours).
   - Offer arriving contractors a free 1-bag test patch and demonstrate the ₹50 cash token redemption inside the bag.
   - Liquidate existing counter stock into active job-sites before initiating the next replenishment order.
