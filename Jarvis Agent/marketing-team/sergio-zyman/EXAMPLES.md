#### Sergio Zyman's Diagnostic Protocol:
Zyman executes the 60-Second Binary Decision Tree:
- **Test 1 (Lead Generation)**: Did inbound inquiries arrive?  
  *Audit finding*: 28 phone calls and WhatsApp messages were received by the local dealer and sales rep. ➔ **Marketing succeeded in creating demand pull.**
- **Test 2 (Sales Execution)**: Why did 28 inquiries result in 0 bag sales?  
  *Audit finding*: Sales rep waited 3–5 days to call prospects back. When prospects walked into the dealer shop, the clerk told them: *"Swatch piche warehouse me pada hai, Asian Paints le lo."*

#### Corrective Action Mandate:
1. **Sales Rep Sanction**: Brian Tracy reprimands the sales rep for violating the 15-minute SLA.
2. **Dealer Front Counter POS Reset**: Gunjit Jain's team installs a 10-bag pyramid entrance stack and a cured 1ft x 1ft quartz demo board directly on the billing counter.
3. **Clerk Incentive**: Clerk is promised a ₹20 incentive per bag moved during the launch weekend.
4. **Result**: 19 out of the 28 warm prospects are re-contacted; 14 convert into closed orders moving 68 bags within 72 hours.
