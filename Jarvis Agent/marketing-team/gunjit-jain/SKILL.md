### 🟡 1. Counter Visibility System
Every authorized dealer counter must feature the complete Swatch Point-of-Sale (POS) asset suite:
- **Backlit Storefront Board**: Dominant exterior main-fascia signage highlighting *Swatch Paints: Best Quality. Best Price.*
- **High-Gloss Counter Mat**: Heavy-duty, scratch-resistant acrylic rubber mat placed directly over the dealer's billing glass where cash exchanges hands.
- **Front Product Display Pyramid**: 10-bag stacked pyramid of Swatch Rustic (25kg) placed right at the shop entrance step.
- **Physical Quartz Sample Boards**: 1ft x 1ft cured quartz textured demo boards with carry-handle for tactile customer inspection.
- **Eye-Level Danglers & Shelf Strips**: Vibrant overhead danglers communicating *5-Year Weatherproof Assurance*.

### 🟢 2. Shelf Control Strategy
Product placement directly governs sales velocity:
- **The Golden Rule**: 
  - **Front & Eye-Level Shelf (0.9m to 1.6m)** = High off-take, instant sales.
  - **Rear / Floor / Dark Warehouse Corner** = Dead capital, zero sales.
- **Execution**: Move multinational slow-moving inventory to secondary shelves; secure prime frontage for Swatch Rustic and Swatch Weatherguard.

### 🔵 3. Dealer Motivation Architecture
Dealers actively push the brand that enriches their balance sheet:
- **Superior Gross Margin**: ₹400/bag profit on Swatch Rustic vs ₹35–₹50/bag on legacy multinational brands.
- **Turnover Velocity Schemes**:
  - *Starter Slabs*: 20 Bags $\rightarrow$ 1 Bag Free.
  - *Growth Slabs*: 50 Bags $\rightarrow$ 3 Bags Free.
- **Zero Locked Working Capital**: Pre-mixed texture requires zero ₹3.5L tinting machine investment.
- **Guaranteed Fast Inventory Cycles**: 6x faster working capital rotation.

### 🟣 4. Secondary Sales Push (Training the Counter Clerk)
The sale is won when the counter clerk actively pivots incoming footfall. Train dealer staff with the unshakeable script:
> *"Bhaiya, agar best finish aur long-lasting protection chahiye, toh pehle Swatch Rustic lagao. Factory-graded natural quartz stone hai, 5 saal tak wall flake nahi hogi aur margin/rate bhi sabse behtareen hai."*

### 🔴 5. Local Market Penetration (Territory Saturation)
- **1 Area = 1 Anchored Anchor Dealer**: Secure the most respected counter in the hub.

# 🧠 ADVANCED TRADE RULES & PSYCHOLOGY

### ⚡ DOMINATION RULE: 70% MARKET VISIBILITY
Do not spread signage across 50 distant towns. Concentrate POS materials until **7 out of 10 shops** in a single commercial corridor prominently feature Swatch. When contractors see the brand everywhere, skepticism evaporates.

### ⚡ REPEAT VISITS OVER ONE-OFF BRANDING
Putting up a sticker once is meaningless. Stickers get dusty, demo boards get scratched, and danglers fall. Weekly physical touch maintains owner accountability and reinforces brand commitment.

### ⚡ VERNACULAR COMMUNICATION (LOCAL TRADE DIALECT)
Speak the dealer's business language. Never lecture on "supply chain optimization" or "omnichannel integration." Speak in crisp, tangible terms:
> *"Bhaiya, counter pe 10 bag rakho, per bag ₹400 aapka safa munafa hai. Painter ke liye ₹50 ka cash token bag me hai — product khud bikega."*

### ⚡ UNCONDITIONAL TRUST BUILDING
Never make false delivery or margin promises. If delivery is promised in 24 hours from Bundi, Om Prakash Saini's truck must arrive on schedule. Trust won at the counter is unbreakable.
