### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory trade marketing cadences, retail point-of-sale (POS) installation protocols, shelf control rules, and secondary sales enablement procedures for Swatch Paints across all Rajasthan trade corridors.

---

### 2. POS Collateral Kit Standards
Every authorized dealer counter must receive and display the standardized **Swatch Trade Visibility Kit**:
1. **Fascia Signage / Front Glow Sign Board**:
   - Minimum dimensions: 6ft x 3ft exterior aluminum composite panel or high-intensity backlit flex.
   - Text: *Swatch Paints — Best Quality. Best Price. 5-Year Weatherproof Assurance.*
2. **Billing Counter Mat**:
   - 2ft x 1.5ft non-slip, scratch-resistant acrylic rubber mat placed directly over cash glass.
   - Contrasting Swatch Rustic natural stone swatch panel illustration.
3. **Entrance Bag Display Pyramid**:
   - 10 ready-to-ship bags of Swatch Rustic (25kg) placed at shop entrance, clean and shrink-wrapped.
4. **Tactile Quartz Demo Boards**:
   - 1ft x 1ft cured exterior texture sample board with ergonomic handle and stainless steel trowel scratch strip.
5. **Overhead Danglers & Shelf Strips**:
   - Double-sided laminated eye-level danglers hung at 1.8m height above main pathway.

### 3. Daily Field Cadence & Inspection Checkpoints
- **Beat Quotas**:
  - **10 Retail Store Visits** per field executive per day.
  - **5 Comprehensive Branding Inspections** verifying cleanliness and product positioning.
  - **3 New POS Collateral Installations** (Counter mats, demo boards, exterior banners).
  - **2 Live On-Site Demonstrations** using scratch trowel in front of visiting contractors.
- **Reporting Schedule**:
  - 17:30 IST: Upload photos of all 3 new installations and audit scores to field supervisor.
  - 19:00 IST: Log all dealer stock counts and secondary sales off-take numbers into master CRM.

---

### 4. Shelf Control & Stock Repositioning Rules
1. **Front-and-Center Mandatory Rule**: If Swatch bags are detected behind competitor emulsion buckets or inside dark godowns, the field executive must physically assist the dealer in shifting a minimum of 10 bags to the storefront counter step.
2. **Dust & Presentation Protocol**: Every visit must include wiping down display bags and cleaning quartz demo boards. A dusty product signals slow-moving inventory to contractors.
3. **First-In First-Out (FIFO) Rotation**: Inspect batch date codes stamped by Chemist Shahrukh bhai; rotate older stock forward to maintain maximum freshness.

---

### 5. Secondary Sales Enablement (Clerk Coaching)
Field executives must spend 15 minutes per visit training the counter clerk using the **30-Second Swatch Recommendation Script**:
> *"Sir, if a homeowner asks for long-lasting exterior protection, tell them: 'Bhaiya, Swatch Rustic lagaiye. Pre-graded natural quartz stone base hai, 5 saal tak wall flake nahi hogi aur margin/rate bhi sabse solid hai.' "*

### 6. Dealer Visibility Scorecard Audit Procedure
Every visited counter receives an immediate score from 1 to 4:
- **Criteria 1**: Exterior / Entrance Branding Visible (Yes = 1 / No = 0)
- **Criteria 2**: Front Counter Product Stack Present (Yes = 1 / No = 0)
- **Criteria 3**: Active Dealer / Clerk Push (High = 1 / Low = 0)
- **Criteria 4**: Painter & Contractor Walk-In Demand Pull (Yes = 1 / No = 0)

Counters scoring $\le 2$ trigger an immediate intervention beat within 48 hours.
