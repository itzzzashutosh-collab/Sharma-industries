### 3. The 3-Step Sensory Persuasion Protocol (Counter Demonstration)
When presenting Swatch Rustic to a prospective homeowner or dealer, field reps must execute the 3-step sensory sequence:
- **Step 1 (Tactile Texture Hand-Off)**: Hand the buyer a 1ft x 1ft cured quartz sample board. Instruct them to run their palm over the stone ridges. *Psychological trigger: Tactile ownership endowment effect.*
- **Step 2 (The Steel Scratch Challenge)**: Hand the buyer a stainless steel key or trowel. Encourage them to aggressively scrape the surface. When zero particles dislodge, state: *"Sir, ye weather aur scratching dono se deewar ko lock kar deta hai."*
- **Step 3 (The Water Droplet Test)**: Drizzle water across the surface; demonstrate zero absorption and rapid beading runoff.
