# Detailed Real-World Case Examples (Indian Paint Trade Context)
## Agent: Rory Sutherland (Vice Chairman of Behavioral Science & Perceptual Value)

---

### Case Example 1: Reframing 25kg Texture Coating as "Liquid Carved Stone"

#### Context & Market Scenario:
Dealers described Swatch Rustic as "bina machine wala sasta texture" (cheap texture without tinting machine).

#### Rory Sutherland's Strategic Execution:
Rory reframes the entire narrative: "Swatch is not paint. It is crushed natural Bundi silica quartz stone suspended in pure acrylic binder. You are not painting a wall; you are cladding your home in crushed granite rock that never fades."

#### Tangible Commercial Impact for Swatch Paints:
Homeowners happily pay Rs 1,150 MRP per bag, perceiving it as an affordable alternative to Rs 150/sq ft Italian stone cladding.

### Case Example 2: The Weight & Tactile Heuristic: The Heavy Sample Board

#### Context & Market Scenario:
Sales reps carried thin cardboard color shade cards that felt cheap and flimsy.

#### Rory Sutherland's Strategic Execution:
Rory mandates hard 1ft x 1ft cured concrete boards with deep aggregate relief weighing 2.5 kg. The substantial physical weight unconsciously signals structural permanence, durability, and luxury to the human brain.

#### Tangible Commercial Impact for Swatch Paints:
Dealers immediately treat the sample board with respect, keeping it prominently displayed on their front desk.

### Case Example 3: The "Costless Luxury" Contrast: Home Interior Accent Walls

#### Context & Market Scenario:
Homeowners resistant to textured finish due to budget constraints for the whole exterior.

#### Rory Sutherland's Strategic Execution:
Rory suggests marketing Swatch Rustic as a single accent wall feature for master bedrooms and living rooms: "Spend just Rs 2,500 on 2 bags to create a five-star hotel lobby feature wall behind your TV unit."

#### Tangible Commercial Impact for Swatch Paints:
Opens an entirely new residential interior market with zero additional manufacturing investment.

### Case Example 4: Eliminating the "New Brand" Cognitive Bias Through Place of Origin Pride

#### Context & Market Scenario:
Contractors skeptical of non-MNC brands produced locally.

#### Rory Sutherland's Strategic Execution:
Rory leverages the Pratfall Effect and regional pride: "MNC paints import synthetic calcium carbonate. Swatch is born in Bundi — the geological heartland of India's finest silica quartz. Engineered for our blazing 48-degree summers."

#### Tangible Commercial Impact for Swatch Paints:
Transforms regional manufacturing from an apparent weakness into an overwhelming localized strength.

### Case Example 5: The Sealed Painter Token Envelope Mystery

#### Context & Market Scenario:
Painters treat discounts casually and lose interest.

#### Rory Sutherland's Strategic Execution:
Rory designs the Painter Growth Token inside the bag as an embossed golden security pouch containing a crisp Rs 50 note and a tamper-evident serial scratch code. The physical tactile unboxing creates a dopamine loop upon tearing open every bag.

#### Tangible Commercial Impact for Swatch Paints:
Painters actively seek out and demand Swatch bags from retailers to experience the opening ritual.
