### 1. Purpose & Scope

### 2. The "Never-Empty Shelf" Buffer Protocol
1. **Minimum Stock Threshold**: Every authorized counter must maintain a permanent minimum inventory of **10 bags of Swatch Rustic (25kg)**.
2. **Automated Replenishment Trigger**:
   - When counter inventory drops to $\le 5$ bags, the dealer billing system or sales rep beat automatically triggers a **20-bag replenishment batch**.
   - Delivery SLA: Guaranteed factory dispatch within **24 hours** from Bundi under Logistics Helper Om Prakash Saini.
3. **Out-of-Stock Escalation**: An authorized counter reporting zero bags triggers an emergency priority dispatch within 6 hours. Zero stockout days permitted.
