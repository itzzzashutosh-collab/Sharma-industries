### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the mandatory protocols for diagnosing prospect awareness levels, delivering stage-calibrated messaging scripts, preventing premature sales pitches, and synchronizing direct-response collateral across all marketing and sales channels of Swatch Paints.

---

### 2. The 60-Second Awareness Diagnostic Protocol
Before delivering any sales presentation, representative must ask 2 diagnostic questions to determine the prospect's stage:

1. **Diagnostic Question 1 (To Dealers)**:
   - *"Sir, aapke counter pe jab customer exterior deewar ke liye aata hai, toh major demand paint ki hoti hai ya log stone texture/coating ke baare me poochte hain?"*
   - *If Answer*: "Sirf paint mangte hain" $\rightarrow$ **Stage 1 (Unaware)** or **Stage 2 (Problem Aware)**.
   - *If Answer*: "Texture ka poochte hain lekin rate zyada lagta hai" $\rightarrow$ **Stage 3 (Solution Aware)**.
   - *If Answer*: "Swatch ka pata hai, kaisa chal raha hai?" $\rightarrow$ **Stage 4 (Product Aware)**.

2. **Diagnostic Question 2 (To Homeowners)**:
   - *"Sir, aapne exterior elevation ke liye paint choose kiya hai ya natural stone monolithic finish plan kar rahe hain?"*

---

### 3. Stage-Specific Message Deployment Standards
Once the stage is confirmed, representatives must strictly deploy the corresponding script:

- **Stage 1 (Unaware)**:
  - *Mandatory Hook*: Paint fading and moisture flaking problem under Rajasthan heat.
  - *Prohibited*: Never mention brand name, bag pricing, or starter offers.
- **Stage 2 (Problem Aware)**:
  - *Mandatory Hook*: Failure of thin paint films; necessity of a 2mm natural quartz monolithic layer.
  - *Prohibited*: Quoting commercial dealer slabs.
- **Stage 3 (Solution Aware)**:
  - *Mandatory Hook*: Economic and durability contrast (Stone cladding @ ₹150 vs Swatch @ ₹25; MNC 3% margin vs Swatch ₹400/bag profit).
- **Stage 4 (Product Aware)**:
  - *Mandatory Hook*: Deploy social proof portfolio (100+ active thekedars, 50+ sites) + 7-day risk-reversal guarantee.
- **Stage 5 (Most Aware)**:
  - *Mandatory Hook*: Present the Shubh Aarambh starter trial (20 bags + 1 bag free + demo board) and close immediately.
  - *Prohibited*: Never re-educate or delay transaction.
