### 🔴 1. Stage 1: The Unaware Prospect
- **Prospect State**: The property owner or dealer does not realize they have a problem. The dealer thinks 3% MNC margins are "normal," and the homeowner thinks exterior paint flaking every 2 years is inevitable.
- ❌ **Disastrous Mismatched Pitch**: *"Sir, Swatch Rustic ka 20 bag ka order le lo, offer chal raha hai."* (Immediate resistance and rejection).
- 🎯 **Stage Objective**: Unveil the hidden cost of the status quo.
- ✅ **Calibrated Schwartz Message**:
  > *"Sir, kya aapne notice kiya hai ki Rajasthan ki tez dhoop me normal paint sirf 2 saal me fade ho jata hai aur deewar se papdi banke girne lagta hai? Aajkal samajhdaar homeowners ordinary paint chorh kar permanent natural stone coating pe switch kar rahe hain taaki 5 saal tak repaint ka kharch zero ho jaye."*

### 🟠 2. Stage 2: The Problem Aware Prospect
- **Prospect State**: The homeowner knows their exterior facade looks dull and cracked; the dealer knows their store is barely making 3% margin on multinational paint. But they don't know what modern solution exists.
- ❌ **Disastrous Mismatched Pitch**: Dumping chemical specs or quoting bag pricing.
- 🎯 **Stage Objective**: Introduce the category of Factory-Fresh Natural Quartz Stone Coating.
- ✅ **Calibrated Schwartz Message**:
  > *"Sir, is moisture aur cracking ka solution ordinary paint ya putty me hai hi nahi — wo to 6 mahine me dubara kharab ho jayegi. Iske liye modern architectural practice me **Natural Quartz Coating System** use hota hai, jo deewar pe 2mm ki monolithic stone layer bana kar paani aur dhoop ko permanently block kar deta hai."*

### 🟡 3. Stage 3: The Solution Aware Prospect
- **Prospect State**: The buyer knows texture coatings and exterior cladding exist, but they are weighing options (local acrylic putty, cement texture, Asian Paints Apex Duracast, stone cladding).
- ❌ **Disastrous Mismatched Pitch**: Re-explaining why stone texture is good.
- 🎯 **Stage Objective**: Differentiate Swatch Paints on unit economics, durability, and factory-fresh formulation.
- ✅ **Calibrated Schwartz Message**:
  > *"Sharma ji, market me 2–3 options hain — ya toh Dholpur stone cladding lagao ₹150/sqft pe, ya multinational brands ka machine-tinted texture lagao jisme dealer ko sirf 3% bachta hai. Swatch Paints Bundi factory se direct pre-mixed washed quartz deta hai — same European luxury finish milti hai 80% kam rate pe, aur aapke counter pe har bag pe seedha ₹400 ka saaf munafa chhoot-ta hai."*

### 🟢 4. Stage 4: The Product Aware Prospect
- **Prospect State**: The dealer or contractor knows Swatch Paints, understands the ₹400 margin and ₹50 token, but hesitates due to brand newness or fear of slow counter rotation.
- ❌ **Disastrous Mismatched Pitch**: Giving long technical explanations about polymer chemistry.
- 🎯 **Stage Objective**: Deploy overwhelming Social Proof, Authority, and Risk Reversal.
- ✅ **Calibrated Schwartz Message**:
  > *"Mukesh ji, Swatch koi naya experimental product nahi hai — Hadoti me already 100+ professional painting thekedars isko apply kar chuke hain aur 50+ commercial sites pe zero complaint record hai. Bundi factory me har batch Senior Chemist Shahrukh bhai lab me test karke dispatch karte hain. Risk zero hai — 7-day exchange guarantee ke sath trial chalu hota hai."*

### 🔵 5. Stage 5: The Most Aware Prospect
- **Prospect State**: The dealer is completely convinced, likes the margin, trusts the quality, and is holding their pen.
- ❌ **Disastrous Mismatched Pitch**: Continuing to talk, educate, or explain (talking past the close).
- 🎯 **Stage Objective**: State the irresistible starter transaction and close immediately.
- ✅ **Calibrated Schwartz Message**:
  > *"Ramesh ji, sab kuch clear hai. Shubh Aarambh starter trial me 20 bags order kijiye, 1 bag hamari taraf se free promotional display hai, 1ft x 1ft cured demo board shop counter ke liye complimentary hai, aur Sunday 6 PM tak 2% cash discount buffer unlock hai. Billing address Bundi chowk ka hi confirm karein?"*
