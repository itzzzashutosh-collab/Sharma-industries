### 🟡 1. Reciprocity (Give Value Before Demanding Business)
Human psychology demands repayment when genuine, unsolicited value is received:
- **Swatch Implementation**:
  - Provide authorized dealers with a complimentary, beautifully framed 1ft x 1ft cured quartz sample board ($₹350$ factory production cost) with zero strings attached.
  - Offer a free on-site application demo on a thekedar's active project wall.
  - Treat visiting painting thekedars to hot counter tea and address them with genuine professional dignity (`"[Name] ji"`).
- **Subconscious Outcome**: The dealer and contractor feel an innate psychological debt: *"Sharma Industries ne bina maange itna support diya, inka 20 bag ka order toh banta hai."*

### 🟢 2. Social Proof (Herd Validation & Risk Elimination)
When uncertain, buyers look at what their peers are doing:
- **Swatch Implementation**:
  - *"Already 100+ professional painting thekedars in Hadoti are applying Swatch Rustic this season."*
  - Maintain a laminated photo binder and WhatsApp business catalog displaying active residential bungalow elevations across Bundi, Kota, Talera, and Baran.
  - Reference known peer counters: *"Bundi bypass pe Sharma Hardware aur Talera chowk pe Verma ji ne already 50-50 bags ka reorder lagaya hai."*
- **Subconscious Outcome**: Eradicates the fear of being the "guinea pig."

### 🔵 3. Authority (Institutional Pedigree & Technical Mastery)
People follow established experts who exhibit quiet confidence:
- **Swatch Implementation**:
  - Showcase the Bundi manufacturing plant pedigree (Sharma Industries, est. regional production, Senior Chemist Shahrukh bhai's precise polymer grading).
  - Explain technical product performance in simple, authoritative terms: *5-Year Weatherproof hydrophobic acrylic barrier with zero efflorescence*.
  - Professional demonstration: Clean, high-grade trowel application executed with absolute precision.
- **Subconscious Outcome**: The counter owner feels safe entrusting their reputation to an established manufacturer.

### 🟣 4. Scarcity & Urgency (Loss Aversion Driver)
Opportunities appear exponentially more valuable when availability is limited:
- **Swatch Implementation**:
  - **Territory Exclusivity Quota**: *"Sir, hum har commercial hub me sirf 2 authorized dealers ko primary distribution rights aur protected margin de rahe hain. 1 slot already lock ho chuka hai."*
  - **Launch Allocation Deadline**: *"Shubh Aarambh launch batch offer Sunday shaam 6:00 PM tak valid hai — agle batch se factory direct ₹690 slab strictly enforce hoga."*
- **Subconscious Outcome**: The dealer fears losing the competitive advantage to their rival down the street.

### 🔴 5. Commitment & Consistency (The Foot-in-the-Door Heuristic)
Once a person takes a small public stance, they strive to act consistently with it:
- **Swatch Implementation**:
  - Never push a 200-bag truckload on day one.
  - Anchor the micro-commitment: *"Sir, pura godown bharne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Sirf 10 bag ka Shubh Aarambh starter trial counter pe rakhiye. Ek site pe thekedar ko test karaiye."*
  - Once the 10 bags rotate and the dealer pockets ₹4,000 net profit, ordering 50 bags is merely remaining consistent with their initial successful decision.

### 🟠 6. Liking (Rapport, Respect & Cultural Connection)
People buy from those they like, trust, and feel culturally connected to:
- **Swatch Implementation**:
  - Authentic, respectful Hadoti/Rajasthani etiquette: Always address partners as `"[Name] ji"`.
  - Zero arrogant corporate posturing. Speak with grounded humility (*Imandari aur Apnapan*): *"Sir hum simple kaam karte hain — quality dete hain, margin dete hain, aur aapka sath dete hain."*
  - Active listening: Let the dealer air their frustrations about multinational brand squeeze before introducing Swatch.
