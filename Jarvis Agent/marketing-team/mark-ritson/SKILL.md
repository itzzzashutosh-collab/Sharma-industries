### 🔥 The 6-Step Ritson Strategic Framework

#### Step 1: Forensic Market Diagnosis
- **Ground Reality Over Corporate Opinion**: Conduct empirical audits across Rajasthan trade corridors:
  - *Competitor Analysis*: Asian Paints Apex/Ultima, Berger Walmasta, Nerolac Excel, Birla Opus.
  - *Pricing Gap*: Competitor MRP ₹1,150–₹1,400 with retail dealer margins squeezed to 3–5% after machine depreciation.
  - *The Swatch Wedge*: Zero machine capital requirement, factory-direct dispatch from Bundi, and a guaranteed **40–45% dealer margin (₹400–₹460 gross profit per 25kg bag)**.
  - *Painter Reality*: Daily wage painters hate complex app points; they demand immediate, tangible cash rewards (₹50 token inside every bag).

#### Step 2: Target Market Segmentation & Prioritization
- **What Swatch Will NOT Do**: We will NOT chase luxury glass showrooms in metropolitan Jaipur or fight for premium tinting counters in high-rent malls.
- **Where Swatch Focuses Firepower**:
  - **Geography**: Primary 150km corridor around Bundi HQ (Talera, Kota City, Dabi, Bijoliya, Baran, Rawatbhata, Uniyara, Nainwa, Dei, Khatkad, Deoli).
  - **Channel**: High-traffic mixed retail counters (hardware + cement + building materials + paint).
  - **Decision Maker**: The painting contractor / thekedar (the true specification authority who chooses the brand for 85% of residential elevations).

#### Step 3: Ruthless Positioning
- **The Core Position**:  
  *“High Performance Natural Quartz Facade Armor at Honest Factory Prices with Unmatched Dealer Margins.”*
- **Vernacular Translation**:  
  *“Best Quality. Best Price. Har Deewar Ka Pukka Vishwas.”*
- **The Category Shift**: Shift Swatch out of cheap commodity putty into **Architectural Quartz Stone Cladding** at 80% lower installed project cost.
