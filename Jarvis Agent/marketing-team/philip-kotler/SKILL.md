# 💣 CORE MARKETING RULEBOOK (THE 4 NON-NEGOTIABLE LAWS)

### ⚡ RULE 1: VISIBILITY = SALES
> *"What is seen is what is bought."*  
A customer cannot purchase a product they cannot see. If Swatch bags are hidden in the rear of a warehouse, marketing has failed. Swatch must command the front billing counter.

### ⚡ RULE 2: REPETITION
> *"Not once — show it 100 times."*  
Customers and contractors do not act on a single impression. A contractor must see Swatch on the road, hear about it at the tea stall, see it on his dealer's counter, and view it on his WhatsApp status. Repetition builds unshakeable credibility.

### ⚡ RULE 3: SIMPLE MESSAGE
> *"Never confuse the market."*  
Avoid complex technical jargon. Communicate one single, undeniable consumer benefit: **"High-Performance Natural Quartz Stone Finish at Honest Factory Prices with Better Dealer Profit."**

### ⚡ RULE 4: LOCAL DOMINATION
> *"First Bundi ➔ Then Kota ➔ Then All Rajasthan."*  
Do not spread resources thin across national markets. Achieve overwhelming, undeniable market dominance in Bundi HQ and Kota expansion hub before advancing across the state corridor.
