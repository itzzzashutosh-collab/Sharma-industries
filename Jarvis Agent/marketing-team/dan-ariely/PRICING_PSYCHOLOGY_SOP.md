### 3. The 3-Tier Decoy Pricing Execution Model

Field sales reps must NEVER present a single price or a binary choice (buy vs. don't buy). Reps must always present the **3-Tier Choice Architecture**:

```text
┌─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
│   TIER A: STARTER LOT   │  TIER B: SHUBH AARAMBH  │   TIER C: VOLUME LOT    │
│    (Deliberate Decoy)   │    (Target Choice)      │     (High Anchor)       │
├─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
│ • 10 Bags Swatch Rustic │ • 20 Bags Swatch Rustic │ • 50 Bags Swatch Rustic │
│ • Billing: ₹6,900       │ • Billing: ₹13,800      │ • Billing: ₹32,250      │
│ • No free bags          │ • +1 BAG 100% FREE      │ • +3 BAGS 100% FREE     │
│ • Dealer pays freight   │ • Free Bundi Delivery   │ • Free Bundi Delivery   │
│ • No display boards     │ • Free Demo Board       │ • Backlit Storefront Sign│
│ • Net Landed: ₹720/bag  │ • Net Landed: ₹657/bag  │ • Net Landed: ₹608/bag  │
│ • Dealer Margin: 37.4%  │ • Dealer Margin: 42.8%  │ • Dealer Margin: 47.1%  │
└─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┘
```

#### The Decoy Mechanism:
- Tier A is deliberately structured with zero perks and dealer-paid freight, making the effective cost ₹720/bag.
- Tier B (+1 free bag, free delivery, free demo board) drops effective cost to ₹657/bag.
- The human brain compares Tier B against Tier A and concludes: *"I would be losing money by taking the starter lot. Tier B is the only smart option."*
- Target: $\ge 75\%$ of new retail onboardings must select **Tier B**.

### 4. Verbal Objection Reframing Protocols

#### Protocol 1: "Dealer Says Swatch Price is Too Expensive"
- **Cognitive Bias Used**: Price-to-Yield Micro-Framing.
- **Forbidden Response**: *"Sir hum discount kar dete hain."* (Strictly prohibited).
- **Approved Script**:  
  *“Sharma ji, aap bilkul sahi keh rahe ho ki Swatch ka rate standard bori se ₹50–₹70 extra lagta hai. Lekin zara munafa dekhiye: purana product bech kar aapko sirf ₹25–₹30 bachta hai. Swatch par aap sirf ₹50 extra de rahe ho, lekin samne customer aur thekedar se pure **₹400 se ₹460 ka net cash munafa** kama rahe ho. Mujhe batayiye, ₹25 kamane ke liye dukaan kholi hai ya ₹400 kamane ke liye?”*

#### Protocol 2: "Customer is Confused Between Brands"
- **Cognitive Bias Used**: Asymmetric Comparison & The Decoy Effect.
- **Approved Script**:  
  *“Verma ji, confuse hone ki zaroorat nahi hai. Market me 3 tarah ke options hain:  
  1. Pehla: Local cheap putty — ₹300 me aayegi, lekin 2 saal me baarish me papdi bankar jhad jayegi.  
  2. Doosra: Dholpur stone cladding — ₹150 per sqft lagega, 2 lakh ka kharcha aayega.  
  3. Teesra: Swatch Rustic Architectural Quartz Armor — Sirf ₹25 per sqft me wahi royal stone finish, aur 5 saal ki complete weather warranty.  
  Bataiye, 2 lakh kharch karna hai ya smart tarike se Swatch lagwana hai?”*

#### Protocol 3: "Competitor is Cheaper"
- **Cognitive Bias Used**: Loss Aversion & Risk-Framing.
- **Approved Script**:  
  *“Sharma ji, sasta maal bechna sabse aasan lagta hai, lekin sabse mehnga padta hai. Sasta paint 2 saal me fade ho jayega toh gaali customer company ko nahi, aapki dukaan ko dega. Swatch Bundi factory ka natural quartz stone hai, Shahrukh bhai ki laboratory me tested hai. Swatch bechne ka matlab hai: Zero complaint, 5-Year Weather Assurance, aur permanent customer retention.”*
