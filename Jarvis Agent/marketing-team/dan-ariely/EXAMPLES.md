### Case Example 1: Reframing the "Your Price is Too Expensive" Objection

#### Context & Market Scenario:
A prominent hardware dealer in Kota City objects during an initial sales pitch: *"Bhaiya, local wall putty aur ordinary texture bag ₹450–₹500 me market me mil raha hai. Aap Swatch Rustic ka ₹690 maang rahe ho. ₹190 mehenga hai."*

#### The Wrong (Amateur) Approach:
Sales rep panics and says: *"Sir theek hai, hum ₹630 me de denge ya thoda discount karwa denge."* This erodes company gross margin and signals that Swatch's price was artificially inflated.

#### Dan Ariely's Strategic Execution:
1. **Psychological Principle**: Price-to-Yield Contrast Framing.
2. **The Script Execution**:  
   *“Sharma ji, aapki baat 100% sahi hai ki local bori ₹500 me aati hai aur Swatch ₹690 me. Lekin zara munafa compare kijiye:  
   - Local bori bechkar aap kitna kamate ho? MRP ₹550 hai, aapko mushkil se **₹30 se ₹50** bachta hai.  
   - Swatch Rustic ka MRP ₹1,150 hai. Aap ₹690 me khareed kar aaram se ₹1,100 me bechte ho, yaani ek single bag par pure **₹410 ka net cash munafa** kama rahe ho!  
   Sharma ji, aap yahan ₹190 extra lagate ho aur samne se ₹360 extra munafa ghar le jate ho. Mujhe imandari se batayiye: ₹30 bachane ke liye dukaan kholi hai ya ₹410 kamane ke liye?”*

#### Tangible Commercial Impact:
- Dealer stops arguing about the ₹190 price difference.
- Immediately signs an initial 20-bag purchase order with zero discount granted.

### Case Example 2: Resolving Dealer Decision Paralysis via the 3-Tier Decoy Menu

#### Context & Market Scenario:
In Talera, a multi-brand stockist is interested in Swatch Rustic but is paralyzed by choice, delaying a stocking decision for three consecutive visits.

#### Dan Ariely's Strategic Execution:
Ariely introduces the **Asymmetric Dominance (Decoy) Menu**:
- **Option 1 (Starter Test — Bare Decoy)**: 10 Bags @ ₹690 = ₹6,900. No free bag, dealer pays ₹300 transport, no demo board. Effective landed: ₹720/bag.
- **Option 2 (Shubh Aarambh — The Target Choice)**: 20 Bags @ ₹690 = ₹13,800. **+1 Bag 100% Free** (Value ₹1,150) + Free Factory Delivery + Complimentary 1ft x 1ft Cured Demo Board. Effective landed: **₹657/bag**.
- **Option 3 (Wholesale Volume Anchor)**: 50 Bags @ ₹645 = ₹32,250. +3 Bags Free + Backlit Storefront Glow-Sign. Effective landed: ₹608/bag.

#### Tangible Commercial Impact:
- Option 1 (Starter Test) acts as the cognitive decoy. The dealer thinks: *"If I buy 10 bags, I pay ₹720/bag and get no display. By ordering 20 bags, I get a free bag worth ₹1,150, free shipping, and a free board!"*
- 78% of hesitating retailers immediately choose **Option 2 (20 Bags)**.

### Case Example 3: Neutralizing Cheap Competitor Products (Reframing Cheap as High-Risk)

#### Context & Market Scenario:
A building contractor in Bundi claims: *"Bhaiya, pados wala supplier ₹350 me bori de raha hai. Main Swatch kyu loon?"*

#### Dan Ariely's Strategic Execution:
1. **Psychological Principle**: Loss Aversion & Brand Reputation Risk.
2. **The Script Execution**:  
   *“Kailash ji, sasta maal khareedna sabse aasan lagta hai, lekin contractor ke liye sabse zyada nuksan dekar jata hai.  
   ₹350 wali bori me marble powder aur chalk bhari hoti hai. Baarish aate hi 6 mahine me deewar papdi bankar niche giregi. Jab deewar kharab hogi, toh homeowner factory ko nahi pakdega — wo aapka agla contract cancel karega aur market me aapki badnaami hogi.  
   Swatch Bundi factory ka natural quartz stone armor hai, Shahrukh bhai ki laboratory me tested hai. Swatch lagane ka matlab hai: 5 saal tak wall chamkegi, homeowner 10 naye padosiyo ko aapka naam batayega, aur aapka kaam permanently book rahega. Aap khud bataye, ₹100 bacha ke saal bhar ki badnaami lena chahte ho ya pakka vishwas?”*

#### Tangible Commercial Impact:
- Contractor drops the cheap substitute and orders 40 bags of Swatch Rustic for an active 3,000 sqft elevation.

### Case Example 4: Loss-Aversion Framing on 2% Cash Discounts (CD)

#### Context & Market Scenario:
Dealers across Rajasthan were routinely ignoring the standard *"2% Cash Discount if paid within 7 days"*, dragging company receivables to 38 days.

#### Dan Ariely's Strategic Execution:
Ariely flips the framing on physical invoices from a potential gain to an immediate loss:
- *Old Invoice Framing*: "Invoice Total: ₹13,800. Pay within 7 days to earn 2% cash discount (₹276)." (Ignored by 72% of dealers).
- *Ariely Loss-Aversion Invoice*:  
  **"Discounted Net Settlement Amount: ₹13,524.00 (Valid until Day 7).**  
  *⚠️ Delaying payment beyond Day 7 forfeits your prompt-settlement privilege and incurs an immediate ₹276 penalty loss."*

#### Tangible Commercial Impact:
- Triggers acute loss aversion; dealers hate feeling that they "lost" ₹276.
- 7-day payment compliance surges from 28% to 84%, dramatically accelerating factory liquidity.

### Case Example 5: The Power of Zero — Physical Cash Tokens vs. Competitor App Points

#### Context & Market Scenario:
MNC paint companies force painting thekedars to download smartphone apps, scan QR codes, and accumulate digital points that redeem for gift cards after 6 months.

#### Dan Ariely's Strategic Execution:
Ariely eliminates cognitive and transactional friction using the **Power of Zero (Immediate Free Value)**:
- Sealed directly inside every 25kg Swatch Rustic bag is a **Physical ₹50 Currency Token in a sealed pouch**.
- The contractor rips open the bag on the job-site, takes out the ₹50 token, and immediately hands it to the retail counter clerk for instant cash or deducts it on their next purchase.

#### Tangible Commercial Impact:
- Immediate tactile gratification creates an unbreakable behavioral habit.
- Over 90% of regional painting thekedars demand Swatch Rustic by name at the counter solely to pocket the immediate cash tokens.
