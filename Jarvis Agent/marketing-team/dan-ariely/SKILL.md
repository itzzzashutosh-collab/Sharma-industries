### 🔥 The 5 Behavioral Pricing Weapons

#### Weapon 1: High Anchor Framing (Contrast Bias)
- **The Psychology**: The first number a prospect sees anchors their perception of value. When shown ₹690 in isolation, the brain asks: *"Is ₹690 expensive?"* When shown ₹1,150 MRP first, ₹690 registers as an immediate ₹460 windfall.
- **Trade Application**:
  - *MNC Brand Texture MRP*: ₹1,150 per 25kg bag.
  - *Architectural Stone Cladding*: ₹120–₹180 per square foot installed.
  - *Swatch Rustic Standard Dealer Price*: **₹690.00** (₹27.60/kg; ₹20–₹25/sqft installed).
- **The Cognitive Shift**: Swatch is immediately perceived not as an unbranded substitute, but as **premium stone facade armor at a 40% discount to MNC retail prices**.

#### Weapon 2: The Decoy Effect (Asymmetric Dominance)
- **The Psychology**: People dislike choosing between two extremes (cheap vs. expensive). By introducing a deliberately calibrated third option (the decoy), customer preference is nudged overwhelmingly toward the target option.
- **The 3-Tier Choice Architecture for Rajasthan Hardware Counters**:
  - **Tier A (Starter Test Batch — Deliberately Bare)**:  
    10 Bags Swatch Rustic @ ₹690 = ₹6,900.  
    *Conditions*: No free bags, dealer pays freight (₹300), no point-of-sale display. Net landed cost: **₹720/bag**.
  - **Tier B (Target Sizing — Shubh Aarambh Slab — THE TARGET CHOICE)**:  
    20 Bags Swatch Rustic @ ₹690 = ₹13,800.  
    *Conditions*: **+ 1 BAG 100% FREE** (Value ₹1,150) + **Free Bundi Factory Truck Delivery** + **Complimentary 1ft x 1ft Cured Quartz Counter Demo Board**.  
    *Effective Net Landed Cost*: **₹657.14/bag**. Gross retail dealer margin: **42.8%** (₹492.86/bag profit vs MRP).
  - **Tier C (Volume Anchor — Commercial Growth Slab)**:  
    50 Bags Swatch Rustic @ ₹645 = ₹32,250.  
    *Conditions*: **+ 3 BAGS FREE** + Backlit Fascia Glow-Sign Storefront Board + Priority 24-hour replenishment guarantee. Net landed cost: **₹608.49/bag**.
- **The Behavioral Result**: 78% of hesitating dealers immediately choose **Tier B (20 Bags)** because Tier A makes them feel foolish for paying freight and forfeiting the free bag.

#### Weapon 3: Loss Aversion (Pain of Loss > Pleasure of Gain)
- **The Psychology**: Amos Tversky and Dan Ariely demonstrated that the psychological pain of losing ₹1,000 is twice as intense as the pleasure of gaining ₹1,000.
- **The Script Framing**:
  - *Wrong (Amateur Gain Pitch)*: “Sharma ji, Swatch becho, aapko mahine ka ₹25,000 ka extra profit hoga.”
  - *Right (Ariely Loss-Aversion Script)*:  
    *“Sharma ji, aapke market me roz 3 se 4 thekedar Dholpur stone aur texture dhundte hain. Swatch counter pe na hone ki wajah se aap har mahine kam se kam **₹24,000 se ₹30,000 ka verified cash munafa gawa rahe ho**, jo seedha pados wale hardware store ki jeb me ja raha hai. Ye paisa aapki dukaan se roz nikal raha hai.”*

#### Weapon 4: Price-to-Yield Micro-Framing (The Extra-Fifty Formula)
- **The Psychology**: When a dealer resists: *"Sir competitor ka local putty ₹400 me aata hai, aapka ₹690 me"*, never lower the price. Chunk the price down to the micro-delta and contrast it with the explosive profit yield.
- **The Verbal Weapon**:  
  *“Sharma ji, aap bilkul sahi keh rahe ho ki local bori se hamara rate ₹90 zyada hai. Lekin local bori bechkar aap kitna kamate ho? Sirf ₹25 se ₹30! Swatch par aap ₹90 extra invest kar rahe ho, lekin samne counter se pure **₹460 ka net cash profit** kama rahe ho. Mujhe batayiye, ₹25 kamana smart business hai ya ₹90 dekar ₹460 kamana?”*

#### Weapon 5: The Power of Zero (The Irresistible Free-Bonus Effect)
- **The Psychology**: "Zero" is not just another price; it triggers an irrational emotional surge that dramatically suppresses risk perception.
- **The Swatch Execution**:
  1. **₹50 Painter Growth Token**: Sealed inside every 25kg bag. To the thekedar, this is "Free Immediate Cash" waiting inside the product.
  2. **1 Free Bag in 20-Bag Slab**: Sells out the entire lot because the dealer calculates: *"Pehli bori bechte hi ₹1,150 cash seedha mera net profit ho gaya!"*
