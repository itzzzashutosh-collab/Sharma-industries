### Case Example 1: Rewriting the Technical Application Manual into "The Master Craftsman's Field Guide"

#### Context & Market Scenario:
The existing application SOP was a dry 6-page technical sheet on polymer cross-linking and mesh viscosity.

#### Ann Handley's Strategic Execution:
Ann rewrites it into an illustrated pocket guide: "How to Create Walls That Stand for a Lifetime — A Guide for Respected Craftsmen". Focuses on practical tips: surface priming, trowel angles, drying times, and preventing monsoon hairline cracks.

#### Tangible Commercial Impact for Swatch Paints:
Contractors preserve the booklet in their toolboxes and share it with junior apprentice painters.
