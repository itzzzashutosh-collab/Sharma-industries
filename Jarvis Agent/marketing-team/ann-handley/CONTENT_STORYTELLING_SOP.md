### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the mandatory editorial guidelines, daily content production cadences, visual asset verification standards, and narrative frameworks for all external and trade content generated under Swatch Paints.

---

### 2. The 4-Part Storytelling Blueprint
All published narratives, case studies, reels, and brochures must follow the 4-part transformation structure:
1. **The Specific Problem**: Identify a localized pain point (fading facade, flaking paint, poor dealer profit).
2. **The Relatable Struggle**: Detail failed past attempts with generic paints or low-margin multinational products.
3. **The Swatch Intervention**: Present the exact Swatch solution (Bundi factory-fresh washed quartz formulation, ₹400 dealer profit, ₹50 painter cash token).
4. **The Verified Transformation**: Present photographic or financial proof of victory with exact locations and names.

---

### 3. Daily Content Production Beats
- **Beat 1 (Field Media Capture)**:
  - Every field sales representative must capture minimum 1 high-resolution project photograph and 1 30-second contractor voice/video note during daily store beats.
  - Upload raw assets to central media pool by 17:00 IST daily.
- **Beat 2 (Editorial & Visual Packaging)**:
  - Editorial team packages assets into 2 daily posts (1 Before/After visual comparison + 1 Contractor video clip).
  - Draft captions using the Conversational Human Voice Standard (Zero corporate jargon).
- **Beat 3 (Multi-Channel Dispatch)**:
  - Publish to Instagram, local trade WhatsApp broadcast lists, and Google Business profiles by 19:30 IST.

---

### 4. Photographic & Authenticity Standards
1. **Real Architecture Only**: 100% of images must feature authentic regional Rajasthan construction (brick and mortar, sandstone elevations, real bungalows). Stock photography is strictly banned.
2. **Real Trade Humans**: Features must depict real painters in working attire and real counter owners behind their billing glass.
3. **Location Geotagging**: Every post must reference real geographic locations (e.g., *Bundi bypass, Talera main road, Kota Vigyan Nagar*).

---

### 5. Content Repurposing Protocol
Every verified customer case study must be adapted across 4 synchronized distribution formats:
- **Format A**: 30-second vertical video reel for WhatsApp Status & Instagram.
- **Format B**: High-contrast 2-panel image with short narrative caption for trade groups.
- **Format C**: Laminated 8x10 counter display card placed at authorized dealer billing glass.
- **Format D**: Sales battlecard asset added to field reps' digital sales portfolio.
