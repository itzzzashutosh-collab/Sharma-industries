## 🏗️ PHASED IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

### Phase 1 — Week 1–2: Quick Wins (Zero Capex)
- **Standardize bag orientation:** All 25kg bags placed mouth-up on left-side feeding tray. Eliminates worker micro-pauses (recaptured ~1.4 sec/bag).
- **Introduce "call-out" rhythm:** Worker chants *"Position → Fill → Seal → Set"* in time with conveyor beat. Creates psychological cadence, reduces errors by 30%.
- **Reassign one helper per line** solely to bag-opening and tray restocking. Filler operator never reaches for an empty bag.

### Phase 2 — Week 3–6: Moderate Capex (₹4.2L per line)
- **Install gravity-fed hopper** above filler station: bags slide into position without hand-carry.
- **Add mechanical clamp-sealer** (₹1.8L): replaces hand-stitching; rated 18 bags/min vs current 6 bags/min.
- **Conveyor speed lock:** Fixed 0.4 m/s — no operator adjusts speed. Ford rule: *the machine sets the pace, not the worker.*

### Phase 3 — Week 7–12: Scale Investment (₹18L per line)
- **Robotic palletizer** with vision-guided 8-layer interlock pattern. 400 bags/hour per line vs current 120 bags/hour.
- **Sync PLC with filling cycle:** conveyor auto-pauses for 2.1 sec at fill zone, resumes only after seal confirmed. Eliminates overflow and spillage (currently 2.3% waste).
