### 1. BAG PHYSICS — Weight as Proof, Form as Creed

**Rory:** A 25kg bag fills both hands. Lift it. That density *is* the narrative. Every MNC bag in this price band sits at 15–20kg. Ours is visibly, almost offensively heavy. That heaviness signals permanence — the same way a thick hardcover book feels more authoritative than a paperback, even if the content is identical.

**Jobs:** Strip the bag down to its essential truth. No cluttered graphics. No decorative logos competing for attention. Just the weight of the product speaking through the packaging itself. The bag should feel like an object someone keeps, not discards.

**Synthesis Execution:**
- Bag weight locked at **25kg ±0.5kg tolerance** (no underfilling)
- Outer bag: **Matte black kraft paper with single white "SWATCH" logo centered** — no illustrations, no promises printed, no dealer scheme banners on the bag face
- GSm grade: **≥80 GSM** (MNC standard is 55–60 GSM; this adds ₹3–4/bag cost but creates perceptible heft difference)

- Inner lining: **White food-grade LDPE** — visible when bag is opened, communicates clean/industrial purity
