## STEP 1: THE FRAME-ENTRY CALL (Klaff Pre-Frame + Belfort Opening)

**Rep Script:**

> "Good morning [Dealer Name] ji, this is [Rep Name] from Sharma Industries — Swatch Paints, Bundi, Rajasthan. I'm calling because we've identified [Market/District] as a priority expansion zone and I have a time-sensitive opportunity I want to put on the table. Do you have ninety seconds, or should I send details?"

**Why This Works:**
- **Klaff Pre-Frame:** You're not asking for a meeting — you're offering access to something *limited*. You set the scarcity before they know the product.
- **Belfort Choice Frame:** "Ninety seconds or details?" gives them control while keeping the door open for both outcomes. No pressure, pure certainty.
- **Status Anchor:** "Priority expansion zone" — you're selecting *them*, not begging them to select you.

## STEP 2: HANDLE OBJECTIONS WITH FRAME CONTROL

| Objection | Belfort Tone Control Response | Klaff Reframe |
|---|---|---|
| *"Rates kya hai?"* | "Rate toh aapko MRP se 40-45% margin milta hai — ₹690 per 25kg bag at standard pricing, ₹640 at bulk tier. Lekin pehle ek sawal: aapka current trade turnover kitna hai?" | Flip the frame — price is secondary to profit potential. Make them sell themselves. |
| *"MNC brands already deal karta hoon."* | "Main MNC ke against nahi bol raha ji. Main bol raha hoon — ₹1150 MRP par 3% margin kama rahe hain? Swatch ke saath aap 40-45% kama sakte hain. Same category, 15x profit difference. Kya yeh discussion layak nahi hai?" | Introduce the "Reality Frame" — they're leaving money on the table every day. |
| *"Credit lena hai na?"* | "7-day credit cycle hai ji, fully standardised. Aap jo volume layenge, wahan se bulk pricing activate ho jayegi. Koi negotiation nahi — sirf execution." | Remove ambiguity. Certainty kills haggling. |

## STEP 3: THE CLOSE — BELFORT STRAIGHT LINE

> "Look [Dealer Name] ji, here's the play: I'm placing you in the distributor tier with a minimum trial order of 50 bags at ₹640 each — that's ₹32,000 landed cost. Your MRP realization: ₹57,500. Your gross margin: ₹25,500. Plus, every bag has a ₹50 Painter Growth Token inside — that means 50 painters become your reference network at zero cost to you. Dealers who commit this week get first-priority supply allocation before we lock other markets. Ready to place the trial order?"

**Key Anchors Deployed:**
- **Specific Numbers:** ₹640, ₹1150 MRP, 40-45% margin, ₹25,500 gross margin — cold, hard math removes emotional resistance.
- **Painter Token Utility:** Creates a B2B2C flywheel. Dealers understand this — it's their free salesforce.
- **Scarcity + Urgency:** "First-priority supply allocation" — Klaff's Prize Frame without being pushy.
- **Assumptive Close:** "Ready to place the trial order?" — Belfort's Direct Line. No hesitation.
