**Day 1 (Due Date):** Automated WhatsApp reminder at 10 AM IST with invoice link + cheerful image of Swatch Rustic Texture application. No pressure. Just a friendly nudge: *"Mr. [Dealer Name] ji, your invoice #____ is due today. Happy to help if you need anything!"*

**Day 2 (Early Grace):** Personal phone call from assigned relationship manager. Tone: helper, not collector. *"Ji, we noticed payment hasn't reflected yet — is there any issue we can resolve?"* If blocker identified, resolve same-day.

**Day 3 (Nudge):** Second touchpoint via WhatsApp voice note from same relationship manager. Personalized: *"Ji, just a quick check-in from your Swatch family. Payment due yesterday — please confirm once done so we can celebrate on-time partnership!"*

**Day 4 (Soft Escalation):** Phone call with solution-oriented framing. *"Ji, we want to ensure your credit limit stays active for your next order. Please clear today or let's discuss a partial arrangement."* Introduce **7-Day Respect Pledge** — on-time payer gets priority stock allocation.

**Day 5 (Firm but Fair):** Formal WhatsApp message with overdue amount, late fee notice (₹50/day after Day 7 per Kotak architecture), and direct payment link. Relationship manager calls simultaneously: *"Ji, overdue is impacting your account health. We still value you, but we must follow our process."*

**Day 6 (Final Warning):** Phone call with consequence clarity. Credit hold announced if not cleared Day 7. *"Ji, tomorrow is the last day before we pause further dispatches. We don't want this — please sort it today."*

**Day 7 (Hard Stop):** Account auto-frozen. Zero new dispatches until cleared. Relationship manager sends personalized message: *"Ji, we miss working with you. Clear outstanding to unlock immediately. No hard feelings — this is our discipline."*
