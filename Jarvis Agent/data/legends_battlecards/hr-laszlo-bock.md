### PHASE 1: WHATSAPP AI SCREENING (Automated, Structured, Unforgiving)

**Input Channel:** Candidate submits CV + availability via WhatsApp (+91 90796XXXXX). Bot auto-triggers 4-module adaptive interview.

#### Module A — Energy Gauge (3 Questions, Auto-Scanned for Verbosity & Enthusiasm Signals)
1. "Tell me about a time you convinced someone who initially said no. What did you say? How many times did you follow up?"
   *Signal Check:* Word count >120 = Energy indicator. Short = low effort.
2. "What's the longest you've chased a lead before closing? Walk me through."
   *Signal Check:* Mentions specific days/weeks = persistence DNA.
3. "Why Swatch Paints? Why field sales? Why Rajasthan-based pan-India scaling?"
   *Signal Check:* Product awareness + ambition signals = cultural fit proxy.

**Pass Threshold:** ≥18/25 Energy Score. Below 15 = Auto-reject. No appeals.

#### Module B — Edge Simulation (2 Scenario-Based Questions, LLM-Rubric Scored)
4. "You're in Bundi. A dealer at Kota market tells you, 'I already stock Asian Paints. Your ₹690 bag won't move.' Respond as if I'm that dealer right now. Type your reply."
   *Rubric (0-15 pts):* Opens with empathy (3) → Anchors on dealer margin 40-45% vs MNC 3% (4) → Calls out zero tinting machine advantage (Rustic Texture) (4) → Closes with trial offer (4). Below 10 = No Edge.
5. "A painter in Jaipur refuses to recommend Swatch because he's used to MNC commissions. You need him to switch in 48 hours. What's your exact plan?"
   *Rubric (0-15 pts):* Painter Growth Token ₹50 callout (4) → Demo bag proposal (4) → Volume commitment hook (4) → Follow-up cadence specified (3). Below 10 = No Edge.

#### Module C — Execution Audit (1 Behavioral + 1 Quantitative)
6. "Describe your last sales target. What was it? What % did you hit? How many calls/visits per day did it take?"
   *Scoring:* ≥80% target achievement + ≥25 daily touchpoints = Execution Green. Red flag: vague numbers or <15 visits/day.
7. **Micro-Assignment (Sent post-interview, 2-hour SLA):** "Draft a 60-second voice-note script you'd use introducing Swatch Rustic Texture 25kg bag to a new builder in Bhiwadi. Send it back as audio or text."
