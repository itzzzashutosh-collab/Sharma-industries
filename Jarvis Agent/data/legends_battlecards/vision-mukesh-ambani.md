## PHASE 1: DEALER ACQUISITION (Months 1–3) — 2,000 Dealers

**Dhirubhai Layer: The Personal Bond**
Every dealer gets a dedicated Swatch Relationship Manager (SRM). First visit is not a sales call — it is a relationship call. Bring a gift: branded apron + calendar with Swatch logo. The SRM signs the dealer on a physical card, gets a thumb impression, and takes a selfie at the shop front. This is the *Niyam* — we remember every person.

**Mukesh Layer: Digital Onboarding**
Same visit, SRM installs the *Swatch Partner App* on the dealer's phone. Real-time order tracking, credit limit dashboard, painter token redemption. One QR scan → account created. Zero paperwork friction.

**Unit Target:** 667 dealers/month → 2,000 by Day 90.
**Geography:** Rajasthan (Bundi, Jaipur, Udaipur) → Haryana → UP → MP → Gujarat → Maharashtra (Tier 2 & 3 cities first).

## PHASE 2: PAINTER GROWTH LOOP (Month 2 Onward)

**The ₹50 Cash-In-Bag Program**
Every 25kg Swatch Rustic bag gets a sealed ₹50 envelope taped inside. Painter opens bag → finds cash → scans QR on envelope → Redeems via Swatch Partner App → Instant UPI payout within 60 seconds. No claim forms. No waiting. This is *trust architecture*.

**App-Based Painter Marketplace**
Dealers see which painters buy their paint most. Dealer earns ₹10/bag referral bonus for every painter who orders consistently. Painter earns ₹50/bag. We cut out the middleman commission entirely.

**Target:** 5,000 registered painters by Month 6. Average 2 bags/painter/month.
