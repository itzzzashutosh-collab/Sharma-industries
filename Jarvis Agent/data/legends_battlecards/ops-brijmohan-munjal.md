## 2. SPARE PARTS & PACKAGING — Lean Inventory Engine

- **Spare-Parts:** Maintain max 4-week buffer stock; automate reorder at 2-week threshold. Target inventory turnover: **4x annually**.
- **Packaging (Bags/Labels):** Lock forward contracts with local bag printers (Rajasthan/Gujarat) on **7-day credit**; payment upon QC pass.
- **Zero-Waste Clause:** All vendor agreements include a **waste-recovery penalty** (1% of invoice value) for excess packaging or rejected material.
