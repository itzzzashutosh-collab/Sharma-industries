## PHASE 1: LABEL THE FRICTION (0–25 Sec)

*"It sounds like you've been burned before by brands promising pan-India reliability but delivering regional inconsistency. And it seems like the last time you expanded your shelf, your margin got squeezed between corporate demands and painter apathy."*

**Neuro-Goal:** Mirror their skepticism as a validated experience, not a resistance to overcome.

---

## PHASE 2: THE NEPQ DISARM (25–90 Sec)

Use calibrated "what/How" questions only — no pitching yet:

1. *"What's been the biggest frustration when working with MNC paint brands on dealer margins?"*
   → Wait. Silence. Let them land on "3% margin."

2. *"How does a 3% margin affect your ability to push our products versus what you can move comfortably on your own terms?"*
   → They connect pain to profit.

3. *"What would need to change for a paint brand to actually move the needle on your monthly cashflow?"*
   → They articulate the solution before we offer it.

4. *"On a scale of 1 to 10, how confident are you that a Rajasthan-manufactured brand can sustain Pan-India quality consistency without compromise?"*
   → If <7, follow up: *"What would it take to move that number to a solid 8?"*

---

## PHASE 3: THE REVEAL (Anchor to Their Words)

Only after they voice the gap, present the math **as their answer**:

> *"You mentioned 3% margins killing your push. With Swatch Rustic, your effective margin versus MRP lands at **₹460 per 25kg bag** — that's roughly **40-45%** before distributor cut. Even at the bulk tier of ₹640 for 50+ bags, you're sitting at **₹510 margin per bag**. And here's what's different — your painters get a **₹50 Growth Token** inside every bag. They become your silent sales force."*

**Voss Synthesis:** Deliver this as an observation, not a pitch. *"Based on what you just shared about margin pressure, this structure seems to directly address it."*

---

## PHASE 4: THE CLOSE WITHOUT PRESSURE

*"What would it take to run a test order of 10 bags on the 7-day credit cycle and see how the painters respond on your floor?"*

Then **stop talking.** Silence is the tool.
