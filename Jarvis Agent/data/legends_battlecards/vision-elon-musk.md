## **1. The Core Problem**

Asian Paints counterattacks by offering dealers **5% instant cash rebate + loyalty tiers** on top of their already-competitive pricing. At ₹1,150 MRP, a 5% rebate = ₹57.5/bag. Our dealer margin advantage (40-45% vs MNC ~3%) is neutralised when AP's rebate narrows the effective spread. Beyond 200 km, our 7-day credit model bleeds working capital — ₹2.1 Cr/month dead across 300+ distant dealers. Bundi plant ships 36 hours before demand signals arrive — classic push waste.

---

## **2. Synthesis: Musk 10X Automation × Ohno Zero-Waste**

**Musk Layer:** Solar-powered automated batching at Bundi HQ. Real-time ERP triggers production only after confirmed dealer orders (pull, not push). Target: **zero finished-goods inventory > 24 hours.** Automated bagging, palletising, and dispatch reduce labour cost by 60%, freeing ₹85/bag for dealer incentives.

**Ohno Layer:** Kanban cards replace forecast-based shipping. Eachdealer receives a **digital Kanban QR** tied to their MRP-ledger. When painter sells a bag, QR is scanned → Kanban signal travels back → Bundi auto-batches replacement. Zero overproduction. Zero stockouts.

Asian Paints rebate is a **financial trap** — it locks dealers into volume commitments that create dead stock when paint goes unused. We flip this:
