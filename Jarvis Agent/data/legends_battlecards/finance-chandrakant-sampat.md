## SECTION 1: THE CASH CONVERSION MACHINE (Sampat Domain)

### Unit Economics Lock
| Component | Cost (₹) | Margin (₹) |
|---|---|---|
| Factory Base | 450 | — |
| Landed Company Cost | 635 | — |
| Dealer Price (Standard) | 690 | 55 |
| Dealer Price (Bulk ≥50) | 640 | 5 |
| MRP | 1,150 | — |
| Painter Token | — | 50 (cost) |

**Key Insight:** Dealer earns ₹55–40% margin at standard price. They earn nothing on bulk — bulk is volume play, not margin play. **Never discount bulk below landed cost of ₹635.**

### Payment Discipline Protocol
1. **7-day credit cycle** — hard limit. No exceptions.
2. First-time dealer: **100% advance** for first 3 orders. After consistent payment history, switch to 7-day cycle.
3. **Auto-stop system:** Any dealer with >7 days overdue — warehouse flag locks further dispatch until clearance.

### The Defect Killer — No 60-Day Credit
- Response to dealer credit demand: *"We operate on a 7-day cycle because it means we can reinvest faster than any MNC. Our dealers who respect this cycle earn 40%+ margins. We don't compete on credit — we compete on margin and cash speed."*
- If dealer refuses: **"Then let's find a dealer who agrees with our model."** Never negotiate the term.
