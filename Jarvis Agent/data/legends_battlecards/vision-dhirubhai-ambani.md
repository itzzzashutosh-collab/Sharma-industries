## PHASE 1: RAJASTHAN FORTRESS (Months 1–4)

**Execution:**
- Hire 6 Area Sales Managers (one per district). Each manages 50 dealers.
- Monthly target per AMS: 50 new active dealers, 80% retention rate.
- Dealer onboarding kit: Swatch price card, ₹50 Painter Growth Token guide, first-order credit sheet (7-day cycle), margin comparison sheet vs MNC brands.

**Painter Loyalty Lock-in:**
- Every 25kg Rustic bag contains one ₹50 cash token — redeemable only via dealer network.
- Monthly "Painter Rank Leaderboard" at district level — top 10 painters receive free texture samples + certificate of partnership.
- Dealers collect painter redemption data weekly → monthly reward dispatch from Bundi.

**Saturation Metric per Town:** ≥40 active dealers, ≥200 registered painters, 80%+ retailer repeat order rate within 90 days.
