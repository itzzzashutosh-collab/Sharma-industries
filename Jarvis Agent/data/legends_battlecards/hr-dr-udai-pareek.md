### THE DEFECT WE CRUSH: Dealer Price Pushback Without Margin Defense

**Root Cause (OCTAPACE):** Low Openness + Low Trust. Field reps sell *price*, not *profit architecture*. They lack the autonomy and competence to lead financial conversations.

**Rao Synthesis:** Competency isn't just product knowledge — it's *profit storytelling*. Our reps must operate at the "Applied Competency Level" where they can co-create a business case with the dealer in under 5 minutes.

---

### ACTIONABLE FRAMEWORK: The ₹500 Profit Bridge Script

**Step 1 — Openness Trigger (Oct 30 Seconds):**
"[Name] ji, aaj ek minute diyein. Aapke current MNC roller coat ke 25kg bag pe margin kitna milta hai?"
*(Force the conversation onto margin, not per-bag price.)*

**Step 2 — Competence Display (Oct 45 Seconds):**
Open ledger. Show side-by-side:

| Parameter | MNC Brand | Swatch Rustic |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Bag Price | ₹850 | ₹690 |
| MRP | ₹950 | ₹1150 |
| Dealer Margin % | 3% (~₹25) | **40% (~₹460)** |
| Painter Incentive | Nil | ₹50 cash/bag |
| Tinting Cost | ₹30/bag | **Zero** |
| **Net Take-Home/Bag** | **~₹25** | **~₹410** |

**Step 3 — Autonomy Ask (Oct 15 Seconds):**
"[Name] ji, agar main 50 bag ka trial order leloon @₹640 bulk rate — aapka net profit ₹10,500 extra/month. Sign karein?"
