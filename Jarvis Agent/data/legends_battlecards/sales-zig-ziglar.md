## **THE TRUST LOOP: 4-PHASE SEQUENCE**

### **Phase 1 — Carnegie Foundation: The Respect Entry (Days 1–7)**

Walk in *empty-handed on product pitch*. Lead with curiosity, not closure.

**Carnegie Rule:** *"Become genuinely interested in the other person — and you'll never be alone."*

**Actionable Steps:**
1. **Visit 3 existing dealers in the target pin code first** — ask them: *"What frustrates you most about your current paint supplier?"* Write every answer down verbatim. No defending, no pitching.
2. **Map the trade network** — identify the top 5 painters who influence 80% of purchases in that market. Painters are your silent decision-makers.
3. **Never walk into a dealer's shop without greeting his staff first.** Ask the shopkeeper: *"How long have you been in this business, [Name] ji?"* Listen. Nod. Remember his story.
4. **Leave behind a Swatch Rustic Texture sample bag** — unopened, labeled with your name and number. No pressure. Just presence.

**Key Metric:** By Day 7, you must know each dealer's top 3 concerns by name. If you can't recite them, you haven't listened enough.

---

### **Phase 2 — Ziglar Integrity: The Value Anchor (Days 8–21)**

Now you speak with authority because you've earned the right to be heard.

**Ziglar Mantra:** *"You give before you get. Service is the rent you pay for doing business."*

**Actionable Steps:**
1. **Present the margin truth, plainly:** *"Sir, your MNC supplier gives you 3%. I'm offering you 40–45% on the same class of product. That's not a pitch — that's math."*
2. **Landed cost transparency:** Show the ₹635 landed cost vs. ₹690 dealer price. Let him see his ₹55 minimum margin per bag before any scheme kicks in.
3. **Painter Growth Token reveal:** *"Inside every 25kg bag is ₹50 cash for the painter. That makes your painter love you. That's free marketing inside the product."*
4. **Credit cycle anchor:** *"7-day cycle, no questions asked. You sell, you pay. You don't sell, you return. Simple."*

**Key Metric:** By Day 21, every prospecting dealer must verbally confirm understanding of the margin gap between Swatch and MNCs.

---

### **Phase 3 — Carnegie Empathy + Ziglar Follow-Through: The Bond (Days 22–45)**

Trust is built in the follow-up, not the first visit.

**Carnegie Rule:** *"Make the other person feel important — and do it sincerely."*

**Actionable Steps:**
1. **7-Day Check-In Call:** *"Sir, did the painter open the bag? Did he count the ₹50 token? What did he say?"* This shows you care about the end-user, not just the sale.
2. **Introduce the dealer to 2 working painters in his area.** Create a micro-community. When painters talk to each other about Swatch, you've won.
3. **Bulk Tier Trigger:** After the first 50-bag order, personally call: *"Sir, your next order qualifies for ₹640/bag instead of ₹690. I want you to know before anyone else tells you."*
4. **Problem-First Support:** If a dealer reports a quality issue, respond within 2 hours with: *"Tell me exactly what happened. I'll sort it today."* Never blame the painter. Never deflect.

**Key Metric:** By Day 45, 70% of onboarded dealers must have placed a second order. Second-order conversion = trust proven.
