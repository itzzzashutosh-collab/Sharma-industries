## EXECUTION ROADMAP (Pan-India Rollout)

### PHASE 1: Rajasthan Home Base (Month 1-2)
- **100 Painter Camps** in Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Bundi — free texture application training
- Each camp: 2kg demo bag free + ₹50 Painter Growth Token per participant
- Dealer network activation: 40 dealers briefed on 40-45% margin advantage vs MNC
- Output: 10,000 painter registrations, 500 dealer tie-ups

### PHASE 2: North India Push (Month 3-4)
- **3 regional cultural ambassadors** from UP, Haryana, Punjab — vernacular connect
- TVC broadcast on DD National + local channels (Doordarshan + Zee Yadav + Sony Pal)
- 200 dealer onboarding across Lucknow, Kanpur, Chandigarh, Ambala
- Painter referral scheme: Existing painter refers new painter → ₹100 cash reward

### PHASE 3: Central India + Retail (Month 5-6)
- Tie-up with **Hindustan Paints, Asian Paints trade outlets** — Swatch as alternative section
- Builder association outreach: 50 large builders in MP, Gujarat, Bihar
- Bulk Tier pricing (≥50 bags @ ₹640) promoted aggressively for builder projects
