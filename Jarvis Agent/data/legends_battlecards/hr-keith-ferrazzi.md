# 🍽️ Swatch Paints — "Ghar Ka Khana, Mitra Ki Baat" Contractor Fellowship Dinner Program

## Philosophy: Relate First, Sell Second

---

## THE CORE STRUCTURE (Per Town)

**Frequency:** Monthly, rotating host towns across clusters
**Capacity:** 10 Top Painting Contractors per town (invite-only, no mass invites)
**Format:** 2.5 hours | Evening, 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM | Restaurant private hall or banquet

---

## EXECUTION BATTLECARD

### PHASE 1: PRE-DINNER (Relationship Intelligence)

1. **Rep homework — Before touching the venue**, each sales rep must document for every invited contractor:
   - Name, current paint brand loyalty, key grievance with current supplier
   - Family details (child's name, spouse's profession — Carnegie's "remember their name & interests" rule)
   - Business volume range (bags/month estimate)
   - One genuine personal compliment to lead with

2. **The Invite**: Hand-delivered elegant card + WhatsApp call. Script: *"You ji, we are not inviting you for a meeting. We are inviting you for dinner — as our guest. No pitch. Just good food, good people. Please come."*

### PHASE 2: THE DINNER (Ferrazzi + Carnegie Synthesis)

| Segment | Duration | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcome Circle** | 15 min | Reps introduce each contractor BY NAME to 2 new people (Carnegie: "Give them importance") |
| **Dinner Service** | 60 min | Seated, family-style. NO selling talk. Reps discuss hometowns, festivals, cricket — genuine interest first |
| **"Swatch Moment"** | 20 min | Brief (5 min) demo of Rustic Texture by Area Manager — show, don't push |
| **Open Floor** | 20 min | Contractors speak freely. Reps LISTEN (Carnegie: "Become genuinely interested in other people") |
| **Group Photo + Token** | 10 min | Every contractor receives ₹500 Swatch credit voucher + "Friend of Swatch" certificate |

### PHASE 3: POST-DINNER FOLLOW-UP (Within 24 Hours)

- Rep sends personalized WhatsApp: *"You ji, thank you for coming. I still remember you mentioned [personal detail]. Let's stay connected."*
- Any order placed within 7 days = double Painter Growth Token (₹100 inside bag)

---

## DEFENSE AGAINST ASIAN PAINTS COUNTER

- Dealer margin (40-45% vs MRP) is the talking point — not price. *"Sir, Asian Paints dealer earns 3%. You earn 40%. That is Swatch value."*
- Rustic Texture's zero-tinting-machine advantage = cost savings they can't match at same margin
- Personal relationship > brand loyalty — contractors switch when they feel *seen*

---

## SUCCESS METRICS

- 80%+ attendance rate per invite
- 30% conversion to first order within 14 days
- Quarterly repeat dinner attendance rate >70%
- Contractor referrals: Each invited contractor brings 1 new contractor next edition

---

**This is not a sales meeting. This is a fellowship program.** The orders will follow the relationships.
