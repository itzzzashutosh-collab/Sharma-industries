## 2. FIXING DEALER PRICE PUSHBACK — MARGIN ARMOUR

**Root cause of prior failure:** Dealers argued "MRP too high, I need lower wholesale." No structured margin defence was deployed.

**New fixed-layer margin model (non-negotiable):**

| Layer | Dealer gets | Painter gets |
|---|---|---|
| MRP | ₹1,150 | — |
| Dealer bulk price (≥50 bags) | ₹640 | — |
| Painter token (cash in-bag) | — | ₹50 |
| **Dealer effective margin** | **34.3%** | **Painter 4.3%** |

**The script (Rep says):**
> "[Name] ji, your cost is ₹640 per 25kg bag. MRP is ₹1,150. Even after giving painter ₹50 cash per bag inside the packet, your realized margin is 34.3% on every sale. Compare to MNC dealer margins of 3%. We don't negotiate on price because the margin structure is already locked for your profitability. The only lever we adjust is volume — above 200 bags/month we can discuss a further ₹5/bag concession."

This removes emotional haggling and anchors the conversation on **structural margin**, not competitive price-matching.

## 3. CASH FLOW LEVERAGE MECHANISM

- **Supplier payment terms:** T+1 to T+7 only. No extended credit.
- **Dealer collection:** 7-day credit cycle maintained, creating a cash float window of ~7-10 days where Sharma Industries holds supplier dues but hasn't yet paid them.
- **Reinvested float:** Used to prepay next procurement cycle at discounted rates — compounding the Damani saving each month.
