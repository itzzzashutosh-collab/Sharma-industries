## 🏪 PHASE 1: CARNegie — THE FIRST IMPRESSION (Days 1-7 per Dealer)

### The 3-Minute Counter Entry Protocol

1. **The Greeting That Disarms:** Never lead with product. Lead with *respect*.
   - *"Good morning, Sharma ji. How has business been this week? I noticed your shop is well-stocked — you clearly know your trade."*
   - This triggers reciprocity. A respected dealer lowers his guard.

2. **The Painter-First Lens (Your Secret Weapon):**
   - *"Sharma ji, I'm not here to sell you paint. I'm here to share how the top 10 painters in your area are choosing their product — and why they keep coming back to your counter."*
   - This positions you as an *insider intelligence source*, not a vendor.

3. **Active Listening Trigger:** Ask one question you genuinely want the answer to:
   - *"What's the one thing about your current supplier that frustrates you the most?"*
   - Listen. Nod. Don't interrupt. Capture the pain point verbatim.
