## EXECUTION STEPS (Phase 1 — Months 1-6)

### Step 1: Identify 12 Priority Clusters
Target towns with population 1.5–5 Lakhs, active construction velocity, and zero dominant local paint brand. Focus: Rajasthan (3 clusters), Madhya Pradesh (2), Gujarat (2), Uttar Pradesh (2), Haryana (1), Punjab (1), Maharashtra (1).

### Step 2: Dealer Onboarding Blitz
Deploy 3 Zone Captains per cluster. Target: 8–10 dealers per town within 45 days.
- Offer: First 50-bag order on 7-day credit, no advance.
- Incentive: ₹5,000 display rack + signage kit free per new dealer.
- Target fill rate: 70% of targeted dealers stocking all 4 SKUs.

### Step 3: Painter Capture Engine (Dhirubhai Play)
Every 25kg bag = ₹50 cash reward directly to the applicator inside the bag.
- Objective: 500+ painters per cluster actively recommending Swatch.
- Tool: Issue every painter a branded ID card + recommendation certificate (creates trust and status).

### Step 4: The 14-Day Turnaround Promise
Every dealer must rotate stock within 14 days. Weekly collection visits by Zone Captain. If a dealer hasn't moved 20 bags in 14 days, conduct a floor coaching session or rotate to next candidate.

### Step 5: Volume Slab Escalation (Pending CEO Approval)
Proposed Dealer Tiering:
- Tier 1 (0–50 bags/month): Dealer Price ₹690
- Tier 2 (51–150 bags/month): Dealer Price ₹665
- Tier 3 (151–300 bags/month): Dealer Price ₹640 (Bulk rate)
- Tier 4 (300+ bags/month): Negotiated pricing — escalate to CEO

### Step 6: MNC Gap Exploitation
Print and distribute a one-page comparison flyer at every dealer outlet:
"Swatch Rustic: MRP ₹1150 | Comparable MNC Product: MRP ₹1400–1600 | Same Quality | Save ₹250–450 per bag."

### Step 7: Cluster Supply Chain Lock
Establish one wholesale distributor per cluster (minimum 300-bag/month capacity) who pre-pays and self-stocks. This eliminates delivery delays and builds capital-efficient inventory.
