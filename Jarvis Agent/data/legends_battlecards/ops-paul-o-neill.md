### 1. QUARTZ SILICA DUST CONTAINMENT (Paul O'Neill Domain)

- **Wet Suppression System:** Automated misting at raw quartz loading hoppers. Eliminates airborne particulate before it enters the workspace. ₹8L total install.
- **Air Quality Monitoring:** Real-time PM2.5/PM10 sensors at each zone. Live dashboard in plant control room. Alert triggers at >50 µg/m³ — work stops until cleared.

### 2. ERGONOMIC WORKFLOW DESIGN (Frederick Taylor Domain)
- **Scientific Task Breakdown:** Taylor-style time-motion study on the Rustic bagging line. Optimal cycle time per bag = 18 seconds (current measured: 24s). Gap = inefficiency + fatigue-induced errors.
- **Conveyor-Assisted Lifting:** Replace manual 25kg bag lifts with overhead conveyor + tilting fill stations. Reduces lumbar injury risk by 70%. Capex: ₹12L. Annual injury-cost savings: ₹4.8L minimum.
- **Rotation Pods:** No worker repeats the same motion for >90 minutes. Four-station rotation reduces repetitive strain by 60%. Schedule is fixed — no exceptions.
