# KAIZEN × DEMING PAN-INDIA QUALITY BATTLECARD v8.0
## Swatch Paints | Bundi Plant → National Standard of Trust

---

## The Core Loop: Daily Kaizen × PDCA Cycle

**Premise:** Quality is manufactured at the Bundi plant, proven through data, amplified through painter trust. Every 25kg Rustic bag must leave with zero variance in weight, seal integrity, and texture consistency — or the dealer margin and painter loyalty both erode.

---

## STEP 1: Establish Baseline Control Limits (Deming — Day 1–3)

| Metric | Current Target | Control Limit (UCL/LCL) | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bag Weight (25kg) | 25.0 kg | UCL: 25.3 kg / LCL: 24.7 kg | Digital scale per bag, 1 bag per 10 checked |
| Seal Integrity Pass Rate | 96% | Target: ≥98.5% | Drop-test 5 bags/hour, record failures |
| Texture Consistency (Batch-to-Batch) | ±5% visual match | Target: ±3% | Shahrukh bhai sign-off per batch with reference panel |
| Non-Conforming Bags/Day | <50 | Target: <15 | Quarantine log, root-cause tagged |

**Action:** Om Prakash records all four metrics in a whiteboard control chart at the packaging line. Data is reviewed daily at 5 PM Kaizen huddle.

---

## STEP 2: Daily Kaizen Circle — 15 Minutes, 5 PM Sharp (Eiji)

- **Attendees:** Shahrukh bhai (line in-charge), Om Prakash (QC), 1 rotating floor worker
- **Format:** 
  - 3 min: Review yesterday's control chart — which metric drifted?
  - 7 min: One kaizen idea from the floor — *who has the closest contact with the problem owns the solution*
  - 5 min: Assign owner, deadline (next day), and success number
- **Rule:** No blame. Only process. If a seal failure happened, ask "what in the machine or method changed" — never "who made the mistake."

---

## STEP 3: PDCA Execute & Iterate (Deming Cycle)

| Phase | Action | Owner | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PLAN** | Identify top defect from yesterday's chart | Om Prakash | 1 problem statement written |
| **DO** | Implement 1 small countermeasure (e.g., adjust sealer temperature by 5°C, add secondary seal check) | Shahrukh bhai + Kaizen team | Countermeasure live within 24 hrs |
| **CHECK** | Measure next 200 bags against target | Om Prakash | Defect rate trend direction confirmed |
| **ACT** | Standardize if good / re-plan if not | All | Updated SOP posted at line |

---

## STEP 4: Scale Signal to Pan-India Dealers (Week 2+)

Once the plant hits **≥98.5% seal pass rate for 7 consecutive days**, ship a **Quality Certificate Card** inside every 50th bag:

> *"Swatch Rustic — Batch No. [X] | Weight Verified: [Y] kg | Sealed: [Date] | Bundi, Rajasthan — A Sharma Industries Product"*

This turns every dealer into a quality witness. Painters see it. Trust compounds. Margins stay protected because return rates drop.
