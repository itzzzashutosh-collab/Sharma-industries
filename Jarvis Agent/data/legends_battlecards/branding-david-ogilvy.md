## ACTIONABLE STEPS

### 1. DEALER ACQUISITION — B2B Network Play
- **Pitch Script:** *"[Name] ji, MNC aapko 3% margin deta hai. Swatch aapko 40-45% deta hai — 14x zyada. 7 din ka credit cycle. Factory Bundi se seedha aapki dukan tak."*
- **Bulk Incentive:** ≥50 bags @ ₹640/bag (vs ₹690 standard). Margin jumps to **₹510/bag** — nearly 44%. Make the math visible on a one-page laminated card.
- **Target:** Rajasthan first (home turf advantage), then Jaipur → Delhi NCR → Indore → Bhopal corridor within 90 days.

### 2. PAINTER LOYALTY — "Rang Seva Mission"
- **Mechanism:** ₹50 cash Growth Token inside every 25kg bag. No registration, no app, no friction. Painter collects, redeems at dealer counter.
- **Story Angle:** *"Har bag mein ek token — kyunki jo deewar rangta hai, uski bhi chahat hoti hai."*
- **Rollout:** 10,000 bags/month pilot. Track redemption rate weekly.

### 3. PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATION ON SHELF
- **Swatch Rustic Texture:** Lead hero. "Quartz-based. Zero tinting machine. Ready to use." Position against ready-mix competitors who demand capital equipment.
- **Retail Pack Display:** Every dealer shelf gets a Bundi stone visual + "5-Year Weather Assurance" badge. Factory seal visible on every bag for trust.

### 4. TRUST ANCHOR — "Bundi Se Aapke Ghar Tak"
