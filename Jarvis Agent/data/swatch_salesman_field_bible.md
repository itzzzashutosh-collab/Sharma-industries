### 🟡 STEP 1: ENTRY (Counter Entry & Frame Control)
**Objective**: Establish instant credibility, break the dealer's preoccupation, and win 2 minutes of focused attention.

- **Exact Vernacular Script**:
  > *“Namaste [Dealer Name] ji! Main Swatch Paints se Ashutosh Sharma sir ki team se hoon. Sirf 2 minute ka time chahiye tha aapka — ek bahut zaroori munafey ki baat share karni thi.”*
- **Field Rules**:
  - ✔ **Confidence**: Stand upright, shoulders back, look directly into their eyes.
  - ✔ **Warm Smile**: Project humility, respect, and local Hadoti connection (*Apnapan*).
  - ✔ **Respectful Address**: Strictly NEVER use *"Ustaad"* or generic titles; always use `"[Name] ji"`.
- **Governing Legends**:
  - **Oren Klaff (Frame Control)**: Do not act like a needy peddler begging for an order. Walk in as a professional peer bringing a high-profit commercial opportunity.
  - **Zig Ziglar (Trust Start)**: Radiate honesty and sincerity from the very first breath.

### 🟢 STEP 2: RAPPORT BUILDING (Disarming Defenses)
**Objective**: Drop the dealer's sales guard by taking a genuine interest in their business reality.

- **Exact Vernacular Script**:
  > *“[Dealer Name] ji, dukaan par kaam kaisa chal raha hai abhi? Is season me market me sabse zyada demand kaunse material ki nikal rahi hai?”*
- **Field Rules**:
  - Listen twice as much as you speak (80/20 listening rule).
  - Never interrupt the dealer while they complain about inflation or slow trade.
- **Governing Legends**:
  - **Dale Carnegie (Human Connection)**: Talk in terms of the other person's interests; remember the dealer's name is the sweetest sound to their ears.
  - **Zig Ziglar (Rapport)**: Connect on shared local pride (Bundi / Kota manufacturing heritage).

### 🔵 STEP 3: PROBING (Uncovering the Hidden Margin Pain)
**Objective**: Ask calibrated diagnostic questions that make the dealer realize how much money they are losing with existing MNC brands.

- **Exact Diagnostic Questions**:
  > 1. *“[Dealer Name] ji, texture coatings aur exterior finishes me aajkal kitna margin milta hai aapko?”*  
  > 2. *“MNC brand ki computerized tinting machine lagane ke liye kitna capital aur floor space block karna pada tha?”*  
  > 3. *“Jo stock 30 din se warehouse me dhool kha raha hai, us locked working capital ka kya karte ho aap?”*
- **Field Rules**:
  - Do NOT pitch Swatch yet! Let the dealer verbally express their frustration.
- **Governing Legends**:
  - **Neil Rackham (SPIN Selling)**: Uncover Situation and Problem questions; illuminate the financial implications of low-margin stock.
  - **Jeremy Miner (NEPQ)**: Ask neutral, non-threatening questions that trigger emotional discomfort with the status quo.
