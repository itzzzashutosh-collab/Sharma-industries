- **Base Market (HQ Ground)**: **Bundi, Rajasthan** (Immediate counter saturation and intensive painter onboarding).
- **Expansion Hub**: **Kota, Rajasthan** (Large contractor projects, builder supply, and major building material counters).
- **Secondary Tier-2 & Tier-3 Towns**: Nearby Hadoti and regional hubs (Baran, Jhalawar, Sawai Madhopur, Tonk, etc.).

### Ideal Dealer Counter Profile:
- **Primary Target**: **Hardware + Paint + Building Material Mixed Stores**.
- **Strategic Rationale**:
  - Extremely margin-sensitive (squeezed by legacy monopolies).
  - Faster switching behavior and willingness to test new high-margin brands.

- **Avoid Initially**: High-end exclusive single-brand paint showrooms (heavily incentivized and tied up by legacy loyalty lock-ins).
