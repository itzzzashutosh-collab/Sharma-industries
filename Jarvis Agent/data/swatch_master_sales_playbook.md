# 💣 SALES EXECUTION RULEBOOK (THE 5 GOLDEN LAWS)

### ⚡ RULE 1: ACTIVITY > TALENT
> *"Jo karega wahi bechega."*  
Smart talk without 45 counter visits is zero. The rep who knocks on 45 doors with average skills will always outsell the genius sitting in an air-conditioned room.

### ⚡ RULE 2: FOLLOW-UP = MONEY
> *"80% sales follow-up me hoti hai."*  
Amateurs give up after one visit. Professionals follow up 4 times (Day 1, Day 3, Day 5, Day 10). If you don't follow up, you gave your competitor a free lead!

### ⚡ RULE 3: NO RANDOM SELLING
> *"Har step structured."*  
Every pitch must follow the structured pipeline: Connect $\to$ Diagnose $\to$ Position $\to$ Prove Math $\to$ Close. Winging it is prohibited.

### ⚡ RULE 4: DATA > FEELING
> *"Numbers decide."*  
*"Sir dealer accha laga"* has zero commercial value. We measure: How many bags? What MRP? When is payment? What is the ROI gap?

### ⚡ RULE 5: SYSTEM > INDIVIDUAL
> *"Process follow karo."*  
No single salesperson is bigger than the Swatch Paints system. The process creates predictable, repeatable crores in revenue.
