## The 6 Mandatory Stage Gates
A deal CANNOT progress to the next stage without meeting strict verifiable criteria.

```
[Stage 1: Profile & EB] ──► [Stage 2: Quantify Pain] ──► [Stage 3: Free Test Bag]
          │
          ▼
[Stage 4: Painter Sign-Off] ──► [Stage 5: Starter PO (25 Bags)] ──► [Stage 6: Display & Retention]
```

### Stage 1: Economic Buyer (EB) & Turnover Qualification
- **Objective**: Ensure Sonu speaks ONLY to the shop owner (Lala ji / Seth ji), not the counter helper.
- **Verification Gate**:
  - [ ] Shop Owner Name & Direct Mobile verified.
  - [ ] Shop sells at least 50+ bags of texture or 200L+ exterior paint per month.
  - [ ] Pincode territory checked in Hermes master CRM (`users.csv`).

### Stage 2: Quantify the Margin Bleed
- **Objective**: Show the retailer in exact rupee numbers how much money they lose on MNC paints.
- **Verification Gate**:
  - [ ] Retailer admits current margin is under 6% on Asian/Berger.
  - [ ] Retailer confirms credit is stuck in the market.

### Stage 3: The Contractor Test Bag Drop
- **Objective**: Never ask for an order on the first visit. Drop 1 Free 20kg Swatch Rustic Texture Bag for their top painter.
- **Verification Gate**:
  - [ ] 1 Sample bag handed over.
  - [ ] Contractor name and site address noted for follow-up within 48 hours.

### Stage 4: Painter Application Validation
- **Objective**: The contractor confirms the product is easy to trowel, has zero shrinkage cracks, and uniform marble grain.
- **Verification Gate**:
  - [ ] Contractor statement recorded: *"Maal badhiya hai, haath par halka chalta hai."*
  - [ ] Contractor sees the ₹50 physical cash coupon.

### Stage 5: The Starter 25-Bag Purchase Order
- **Objective**: Secure the initial 25-bag trial stocking order with advance / CD terms.
- **Verification Gate**:
  - [ ] Dealership registration details filled (GSTIN, Shop Name, Delivery Address).
  - [ ] Advance RTGS/UPI payment received (₹16,250 for 25 bags).
  - [ ] PO logged into Hermes `data-store.js`.

### Stage 6: Shop Display Installation & Retain
- **Objective**: Install the metallic Swatch display board and schedule the 14-day reorder check.
- **Verification Gate**:
  - [ ] Photo of shop display uploaded to CRM.
  - [ ] Joe Girard's 14-day follow-up card scheduled.
