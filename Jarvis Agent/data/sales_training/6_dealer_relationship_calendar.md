## 1. Joe Girard’s "Law of 250" in an Indian Paint Market
Joe Girard proved that every human has an average of 250 people in their wedding or funeral circle.
In an Indian Paint Market:
- Every hardware shopkeeper is regularly dealing with **15 to 35 painting thekedaars (contractors)**.
- Every contractor manages **5 to 12 mistris and painters**.
- Therefore, **1 happy dealer directly unlocks 150+ painters and 50+ residential/commercial paint jobs**.
- If you burn a bridge with 1 dealer, word spreads across the entire market lane within 24 hours. If you treat 1 dealer like royalty, their painters become your brand ambassadors.

---

## 2. Dale Carnegie's 4 Market Merchant Commandments
1. **Remember and Honor the Owner’s Name & Family**: Never address a shopkeeper as *"Bhaiya"* once you know his name. Always say *"Radhey Shyam ji"*, *"Gupta ji"*, or *"Sharma Sahab"*. Notice their family photo or puja thali in the cash galla.
2. **Talk in Terms of the Dealer's Profit, Not Your Target**: Never say *"Humara monthly target hai, thoda maal utha lo"*. Instead say: *"Aapki dukan par is mahine ₹25,000 extra cash profit add karne ke liye plan laya hun"*.
3. **Praise Their Counter Standing Sincerely**: Acknowledge that their shop is a trusted landmark in the market (*"Seth ji, is area me thekedaar aapki baat patthar ki lakeer maante hain"*).
4. **Never Argue or Make Them Wrong**: Even if the dealer makes an incorrect statement about paint chemistry, acknowledge and bridge gracefully (*"Aapka kehna bilkul swabhavik hai Seth ji, aur purane products me aisa hi hota tha..."*).
