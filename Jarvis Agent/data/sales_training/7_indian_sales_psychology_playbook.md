## 1. CORE INDIAN BUYER PSYCHOLOGY

### Rule 1: Relationship Before Transaction
In India, sale happens **person-to-person**, not product-to-person.  
Do not try to close in the first meeting. First meeting = build trust.

**Script:**
> “Namaste Sir. I am [Name] from Swatch Paints. How are you? How is business? How long have you had this shop?”

Talk about family, shop, local area. **Business talk comes after 5 minutes.**

---

### Rule 2: Reference Is the Strongest Weapon
Indian buyers do not trust unknown salespeople. They trust **references**.

**Script:**
> “Sir, I came from [Dealer Name] in your area. He said I should meet you. He gave me your name.”

If no reference:
> “Sir, I am also from [same city/community]. I did not know you, but [mutual contact] spoke highly of you.”

---

### Rule 3: “I’ll Think About It” = Soft No
Indian buyers rarely say direct “no.” They give excuses:
- “I’ll think about it.”
- “No budget right now.”
- “We’ll see later.”
- “I’ll ask at home.”
- “Leave your card.”

**Response:**
> “Sir, is there a problem? Price, quality, or something else? Let me solve it.”

---

### Rule 4: Bargaining Is the National Sport
Indian buyers never accept the first price. Bargaining is their **right**.

**Method:**
1. Quote **10–15% above MRP**.
2. Then give **“special price for you.”**
3. Add a free item: brush, cap, t-shirt, diary.
4. Free item feels bigger than discount.

**Script:**
> “Sir, for you I will do a special price. And I will give you a free painter kit. Only for you.”

---

### Rule 5: Family and Community Decide
Decisions are not individual:
- Dealer: wife, brother, son involved.
- Painter: [Painter Name] ji & contractor opinion matters.
- Homeowner: wife, mother, father all have a vote.

**Script:**
> “Sir, please discuss at home. I can also meet Madam and show her shades. You both decide together.”

---

### Rule 6: Trust Signals — Show These 5 Things
1. GST number — “We are a registered company.”
2. Factory/godown visit — “Come see our factory.”
3. Other dealers — “X brother in your area also buys from us.”
4. Certifications — BIS, ISO, GreenPro.
5. Company history — “We have been in this business for X years.”

---

### Rule 7: Emotion Sells, Product Does Not
Indian paint buyers buy **protection, beauty, status, family pride** — not chemicals.

**Script:**
> “Madam, this paint will keep your house beautiful for 5 years with our weather resistance assurance. Your neighbours will ask which paint you used.”

## 2. BUYER TYPES & SCRIPTS

### Type 1: The Dealer
**Mindset:** “Which brand gives me fastest movement and highest margin?”  
**Fear:** “New brand will block my money.”  
**Desire:** Margin, fast-moving stock, credit, exclusivity.

**Opening Script:**
> “Sir, I am from Swatch Paints. We are launching in your area (Rajasthan - Bundi & Kota priority). I am offering you first-dealer advantage. Margin 40–45%, 30 days credit, zero tinting machine hassle (ready-to-apply 25kg bags), and 1ft x 1ft textured sample display boards for your counter. Take one trial order. If it does not sell, I will take it back.”

**Objection Handling:**

| Dealer Says | You Say |
|---|---|
| “Who will buy a new brand?” | “Sir, that is why you get first chance. When everyone buys, margin will drop.” |
| “Asian Paints sells.” | “Asian Paints gives you 25% margin. We give 45%. Who is giving you more profit?” |
| “My money will get stuck.” | “Sir, we give return guarantee. I will write it down.” |
| “I need credit.” | “30 days credit. If you pay in 7 days, 2% extra discount.” |

---

### Type 2: The Painter
**Mindset:** “My work must look good. I earn from recommendations.”  
**Fear:** “New paint fails, my reputation is damaged.”  
**Desire:** Professional respect, cash incentive, training, tools.

**Opening Script:**
> “Namaste [Painter Name] ji, how are you? I am from Swatch Paints. We have the **Painters Growth Tokens** program for professional craftsmen like you. Every 25kg bag of Swatch Rustic has a ₹50 cash token inside, plus slab rewards for volume. We also give you 1ft x 1ft sample boards to show customers on site. Try it on your next site. If you like it, recommend it to your customers.”

**Painter Psychology:**
- Professional respect by name is the biggest incentive.
- Cash must be instant (₹50 token inside each bag).
- Tools & sample boards = identity & craft pride.
- Training = career growth.
- Community = belonging.

**Painter Loyalty Script:**
> “[Painter Name] ji, your craftsmanship deserves the highest respect. Under Painters Growth Tokens program, we will put your name on our verified portal as a Swatch Certified Painter. We will also send direct homeowner leads to you.”

---

### Type 3: The Homeowner
**Mindset:** “My house should look good, but I must save money.”  
**Fear:** “Bad paint means repainting cost.”  
**Desire:** Beautiful home, warranty, low maintenance, value.

**Opening Script:**
> “Sir/Madam, I am from Swatch Paints. I am offering a free colour consultation and live texture demo with our 1ft x 1ft sample board for your home. We give a 5-Year Durability & Weather Resistance Assurance — complete peace of mind.”

**Objection Handling:**

| Customer Says | You Say |
|---|---|
| “Too expensive.” | “Madam, this is one-time cost. Cheap paint fades in 2 years. Ours lasts 5 years under warranty. Calculate — it is cheaper.” |
| “Use Asian Paints.” | “Asian Paints is good, but their dealer margin is higher. We supply direct from factory, so quality is high, price is honest, and you get better durability.” |
| “I’ll think about it.” | “Madam, take this shade card and sample home. Discuss with family. I will come tomorrow. If you decide today, I can give 10% discount.” |

---

### Type 4: The Contractor
**Mindset:** “Work must finish on time. I need bulk discount.”  
**Fear:** “Bad paint delays payment.”  
**Desire:** Bulk discount, fast delivery, credit, site delivery.

**Opening Script:**
> “Sir, how much paint will your site need? We supply direct from factory. Delivery in 24 hours to your site. Bulk order gets up to 50% discount. We also give credit — payment after 45 days.”

---

### Type 5: The Architect / Interior Designer
**Mindset:** “My design must look good. Shade range and quality matter.”  
**Fear:** “Client dislikes it, my reputation suffers.”  
**Desire:** Premium products, shade range, sample kits, commission, recognition.

**Opening Script:**
> “Sir, for your clients we have a premium range — Swatch Rustic. Textured finishes, metallic shades, Italian finish. Here is our 1ft x 1ft tactile sample board. Please touch and feel the build. We also organise architect meets. You are invited. Your clients will get special discount.”

## 3. THE 5-STEP INDIAN SALES SCRIPT

### Step 1: Opening — 30 Seconds
**Purpose:** Build trust, not close deal.

> “Namaste Sir. I am [Name] from Swatch Paints. How are you? [Chai talk]”

**Golden Rule:** First 5 minutes only relationship talk.

---

### Step 2: Probing — 2 Minutes
**Purpose:** Understand their problem.

> “Sir, how many years have you had this shop? Which brand sells most? What do customers ask for? What problems do you face?”

Listen. Write. Understand.

---

### Step 3: Presentation — 2 Minutes
**Purpose:** Solve their problem, not push product.

> “Sir, I understand your problem. You need fast-moving stock and margin without locking capital in expensive machines. Our model: 45% margin, 30 days credit, zero tinting machine investment (ready-to-use 25kg bags). You do not have this right now.”

**Rule:** Only 3 points. Do not over-talk.

---

### Step 4: Objection Handling — 5 Minutes
**Purpose:** Remove fear and doubt.

> “Sir, what problem do you see? Price? Quality? Something else?”

Every objection is a buying signal. Welcome it.

---

### Step 5: Closing — 2 Minutes
**Purpose:** Get decision.

**Indian Closing Techniques:**

1. **Assumptive Close:**
> “Sir, I will send 10 bags (25kg) tomorrow. Keep them on the counter. If they do not sell, I will take them back.”

2. **Choice Close:**
> “Sir, will you start with 25 bags or 50 bags for your counter display?”

3. **Urgency Close:**
> “Sir, this offer is only for this month. Next month margin drops to 35%. Sign today.”

4. **Fear Close:**
> “Sir, I am giving this offer to another dealer in your area. If you take it first, you keep the customers. Otherwise he will.”

5. **Relationship Close:**
> “Sir, it is my good fortune to work with a person like you. Give me one chance. I will never disappoint you.”

## 4. WHAT NOT TO DO — INDIAN SALES TABOOS

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct Way |
|---|---|
| Closing in first meeting | Close in 3rd meeting |
| Speaking too much English | Speak local language |
| Talking technical product features | Talk benefit and emotion |
| Not saying “Aap” / “Sir” / “Madam” | Always use respect words |
| Giving discount first | Quote price first, then discount |
| Refusing chai/water | Accept chai/water — it builds relationship |
| Rushing the buyer | Give time — Indian buyer thinks before buying |
| Praising another customer in front of buyer | Make the buyer the hero |
| Taking cash without bill | Always give proper bill |
| Breaking a promise | One broken promise = trust gone |

## 5. REGIONAL PSYCHOLOGY MATRIX

| Region | Buyer Psychology | Sales Approach |
|---|---|---|
| North: UP, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana | Relationship-driven, heavy bargaining, ego-sensitive | Chai talk, reference, respect |
| West: Gujarat, Maharashtra | Business-minded, value-conscious, time-punctual | Direct talk, numbers, fast delivery |
| South: TN, Karnataka, Kerala, AP | Quality-conscious, brand loyal, education-driven | Technical details, certification, demo |
| East: WB, Odisha, Assam | Price-sensitive, local brand preference | Competitive pricing, local influencer |
| Rural India | Trust-driven, word-of-mouth, festival timing | Panchayat, mela, local leader, sachet packs |
| Metro India | Premium, convenience, digital-first | App, AR visualiser, home delivery |
