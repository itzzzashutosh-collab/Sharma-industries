# Playbook 3: Market Diagnostic Questions (SPIN & Gap Selling)
**Assigned Legends**: **Neil Rackham** (Consultative Selling / SPIN) & **Keenan** (Gap Selling & Problem Discovery)  
**Trained on**: Prime Agent Market Intelligence Dossier 2026 & Rajasthan Hardware Ecosystem  
**Target Profile**: Hardware store owners, sanitary+paint combo shops, and independent paint stockists.

---

## 1. The Core Psychology: Don't Pitch the Bag, Expose the Bleeding Cash
Indian hardware & paint retailers do not buy from new paint companies because of feature lists. They only switch counter space when they realize their current brand portfolio is bleeding cash through:
1. Razor-thin margins (4-6% on Tier-1 brands) where bank interest (12% OD) exceeds margin.
2. 45-60 day contractor credit traps where their own working capital is stuck.
3. Over-saturation where 5 shops in the same lane undercut each other by ₹10-20 per bucket.

---

## 2. Neil Rackham's Market SPIN Sequence (Hindi / English Transcripts)

### S — Situation Questions (Counter Reality Check)
*Objective: Get the dealer talking about their daily footfall and volume without being defensive.*
- **Question 1**: *"Bhaiya, aapke counter pe daily ka texture aur exterior paint ka kitna demand nikal raha hai abhi season me?"*
- **Question 2**: *"Aapke pass jo regular 10-15 thekedaar aate hain, vo mostly standard brands le jaate hain ya unko local/project grade material bhi chahiye hota hai?"*

### P — Problem Questions (Uncovering Counter Frustrations)
*Objective: Tap into the dealer's unspoken frustrations with Tier-1 companies.*
- **Question 3**: *"Aaj kal Asian Paints ya Berger me baaki dukan wale bhi wahi bech rahe hain, to kya customer aapse ₹10-20 ke liye rate compare karke dusri dukan pe chala jata hai?"*
- **Question 4**: *"Aapko company se target pura karne par rebate to milta hai, par kya counter pe bucket bechte waqt haath me ₹50 bhi bachta hai kharcha kaat ke?"*

### I — Implication Questions (Amplifying the Cost of Staying the Same)
*Objective: Connect their current situation to lost cash and working capital stress.*
- **Question 5**: *"Agar aap mahine me 200 bucket badi company ki bechte ho aur margin sirf 5% hai, aur thekedaar aapse 45 din ki udhari le gaya... to bank CC limit ka 1% to interest me hi nikal gaya na bhaiya? Fir dukandaar ke haath me kya bacha?"*
- **Question 6**: *"Agar kal ko kisi project me contractor payment late kar de, to badi company to aapka cheque bounce kar degi ya supply rok degi. Kya counter pe koi aisa product nahi hona chahiye jisme 20-25% cash profit sidha pocket me aaye?"*

### N — Need-Payoff Questions (Leading to the Swatch Solution)
*Objective: Make the dealer articulate why they need Swatch Paints.*
- **Question 7**: *"Agar aapke counter pe ek aisa premium water-based texture aur roller coat lag jaye jisme per bag ₹150-₹200 clear cash bache, thekedaar ko bag me ₹50 cash token mile, aur unbika maal 30 din me 100% factory return ho... to kya aapke working capital par load kam hoga ya nahi?"*

## 3. Keenan's Gap Selling Matrix (Current State vs. Future State)

| Dimension | Current State (Tier-1 Brand Trap) | The Financial Gap | Future State with Swatch Paints |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Margin** | 4% – 7% per bucket / bag (₹35–₹60) | **-₹120 to -₹150 per bag lost** | 20% – 25% net profit (₹150–₹250 per bag) |
| **Credit Risk** | Dealer finances the contractor (45-60 days) | **₹2,00,000+ capital locked** | Low-ticket starter, immediate 3% Cash Discount |
| **Contractor Loyalty** | Thekedaar shops wherever ₹10 cheaper | **Zero loyalty to dealer** | ₹50 instant scratch token pulls mistri back to shop |
| **Price Competition** | 6 shops in 500m selling the exact same SKU | **Price war, margins killed** | Market territory protection (1 exclusive dealer/lane) |
