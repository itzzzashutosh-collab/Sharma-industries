# 2. THE 4-STEP CLOSING CADENCE PROTOCOL

Field salesmen must never pitch randomly. Every conversation follows this 4-step sequence:

```text
CONNECT ────────► DIAGNOSE ────────► PRESENT ────────► CLOSE
(Build Trust)     (Uncover Pain)    (Position Soln)   (Natural Decision)
```

---

### 1️⃣ STEP 1: CONNECT (Build Immediate Trust)
* **Objective**: Disarm defensive counter owner within the first 60 seconds.
* **Rule**: Do NOT mention Swatch Paints or try to sell immediately. Treat the shopkeeper with genuine respect.
* **Field Scripts**:
  * *"Namaste [Owner Name] ji! Aapki dukaan ka display kaafi organized hai. Kitne saal se aap is market me operate kar rahe hain?"*
  * *"Sir, market me aajkal sabse zyada customer demand kis texture aur paint brand ki chal rahi hai aapke counter par?"*
  * *"Sir, main Sharma Industries se hoon, bas market trends samajhne aaya tha — aap do minute ka samay de payenge?"*

---

### 2️⃣ STEP 2: DIAGNOSE (Understand Reality & Pain Points)
* **Objective**: Help the dealer self-realize their margin compression and capital lock-in with MNC brands.
* **Field Scripts**:
  * **On Margin**: *"Sir, MNC brands (Asian/Berger) par aapko texture aur putty par typically kitna margin milta hai? Kya 3% se 5% ke upar kuch bachta hai?"*
  * **On Capital Lockup**: *"Sir, tinting machine aur colorants me jo ₹3.5 Lakh fasa hua hai, kya uska return on investment aapko comfortable lagta hai?"*
  * **On Slow Movement**: *"Sir, kya kabhi aisa hota hai ki texture ka maal dukan me mahino tak pada reh jaye aur contractor nayi supply na maange?"*

---

### 3️⃣ STEP 3: PRESENT (Position Swatch as the Exact Solution)
* **Objective**: Present Swatch as the tailor-made cure to the dealer's diagnosed problem.
* **Field Scripts**:
  * *"Sir, humne Swatch Rustic ko design hi isliye kiya hai taaki dealer ko 3% ki jagah seedha **40% clean margin (₹460/bag profit)** mile — bina kisi tinting machine ke!"*
  * *"Sir, hamara maal 100% natural Bundi silica quartz se bana pre-mixed ready paste hai. Machine ki zaroorat hi nahi hai."*
  * *"Stock slow hone ka sawal hi nahi hai, kyunki har 25kg bag ke andar **₹50 ka cash token** sealed hai — painter khud aakar aapke counter se Swatch maangta hai!"*

---

### 4️⃣ STEP 4: CLOSE (The Natural Low-Risk Decision)
* **Objective**: Close with zero sales pressure using low-risk trial entry.
* **Field Scripts**:
  * *"Sir, hum aapse poora showroom badalne ko nahi keh rahe. Chaliye sirf **20-bag ka trial order** se test karte hain."*
  * *"Sir, hamara **Shubh Aarambh 50-bag package** lijiye — 5 bag Bundi plant se free aayenge, 1x1ft sample board counter par lagega, aur agar 7 din me na bika toh 100% SKU exchange guarantee hamari!"*
  * *"Kya main aapka trial lot kal subah dispatch karwa doon ya parso?"*

* **Tracy 4-Step Execution**:
  * **Diagnose**: *"Sir, agar 50 bag bechne par aapko sirf ₹1,750 bache, toh dukaan ka rent aur bijli ka bill kaise niklega?"*
  * **Present**: *"Swatch Rustic me 50 bag par aapka seedha net munafa **₹23,000** hota hai — ₹460 seedha har bag par aapki jeb me!"*
  * **Close**: *"Sir, aap Asian band mat kijiye, bas counter par 20 bag Swatch trial rakhiye. Apne best thekedar ko dijiye aur margin ka difference khud dekhiye."*

* **Tracy 4-Step Execution**:

  * **Present**: *"Swatch me har 25kg bag ke andar **₹50 instant cash token** sealed hai. Painter jab dekhega ki din me 10 bag lagane par ₹500 cash mil raha hai, wo khud dukan aakar Swatch maangega."*
  * **Close**: *"Saath hi hamari 7-Day Slow-Move Exchange Guarantee hai — 7 din me move na ho toh emulsion se 1-to-1 swap kar lijiye. Aapka zero risk hai Sir."*

### 🔴 CASE 3: The Trust & Quality Objection (Nayi Brand Par Vishwas Nahi)
* **Dealer Statement**: *"Local brand deewar par chhoot gaya ya crack ho gaya toh meri dukan ki saakh kharab ho jayegi."*
* **Tracy 4-Step Execution**:

  * **Close**: *"Aap ek bag apne sabse bharosemand thekedar ko test application ke liye dijiye. Jab wo bol de ki finish A-1 hai, tabhi 50 bag ka lot uthaiye."*

### 🔴 CASE 4: The Price-per-Bag Confusion (MRP vs Dealer Price)
* **Dealer Statement**: *"₹690 bag bohot mehenga lag raha hai. Putty toh ₹350 me aati hai."*
* **Tracy 4-Step Execution**:
  * **Diagnose**: *"Sir, kya aap white cement putty ko architectural quartz texture ke sath compare kar rahe hain? Putty par MRP kya hai aur margin kya hai?"*
  * **Present**: *"Putty par aap ₹30 kamate hain. Swatch Rustic texture coating hai jiska MRP ₹1,150 hai aur aapko ₹690 me milti hai — seedha ₹460 per bag profit!"*
  * **Close**: *"Customer texture ka kaam karwa raha hai toh use putty nahi, premium finish chahiye. Aaiye 20 bag se start karte hain."*

---

### 🔴 CASE 5: Loyal to Competitor (MNC Monopoly Defender)

* **Tracy 4-Step Execution**:

  * **Present**: *"Hum aapko Asian replace karne ko bilkul nahi bol rahe. Swatch ko secondary high-margin product ke roop me rakhiye. Jo customer honest price me high durability chahta hai, use Swatch dijiye."*
  * **Close**: *"Aapki dukaan par 10ft ka ek display stand lagate hain. No replacement, just a complementary trial."*

# 4. 5-STAGE PIPELINE TRACKING & PROGRESSION CRITERIA

Every counter in the salesman's territory must strictly reside in one of these 5 stages:

```text
[1] NEW CONTACT ──► [2] INTERESTED ──► [3] TRIAL DISCUSSION ──► [4] ORDER PENDING ──► [5] CLOSED
```

1. **Stage 1: New Contact**: First visit completed. Counter profile and owner contact details logged in ledger.
2. **Stage 2: Interested**: Dealer engaged in 4-Step Cadence, verified 40% margin, inspected 1ft x 1ft sample board.
3. **Stage 3: Trial Discussion**: Dealer evaluating 20-bag or 50-bag Shubh Aarambh package; painter leads discussed.
4. **Stage 4: Order Pending**: Invoice terms confirmed (₹690 billing, 5-day 2% CD, 7-day credit limit); dispatch date awaiting confirmation.
5. **Stage 5: Closed**: Order billed, trial lot dispatched from Bundi plant, logged into master CRM.
