## 1. Swatch Rustic Exterior Texture Coating (Flagship SKU: SW-RUSTIC-TEX-01)
*Application: Heavy textured protective exterior finish with superior quartz marble grain.*
- **Batch Size Reference**: 1,000 kg Base
- **Raw Material Composition (BOM)**:
  1. Water (DM Water): 160.0 kg (16.0%)
  2. Hydroxyethyl Cellulose (HEC - Natrosol 250 HHR): 3.5 kg (0.35%) — Thickener / Rheology modifier
  3. Ammonia Solution (25%): 1.5 kg (0.15%) — pH neutralizer (pH 8.5–9.2)
  4. Sodium Polycarboxylate Dispersant (Orotan 731A): 4.0 kg (0.40%)
  5. Non-Ionic Wetting Agent (Triton X-100 / Tergitol): 2.0 kg (0.20%)
  6. Mineral Oil / Silicone Antifoam (Nopco 8034): 2.5 kg (0.25%)
  7. Rutile Titanium Dioxide (TiO2 - Tronox CR-828): 35.0 kg (3.50%)
  8. Calcite / Calcium Carbonate Powder (300 Mesh): 220.0 kg (22.00%)
  9. Natural Graded Quartz Sand / Marble Granules (0.5mm – 1.0mm): 380.0 kg (38.00%)
  10. Pure Acrylic Copolymer Emulsion (Primal AC-261 / 50% Solids): 160.0 kg (16.00%)
  11. Texanol Ester Alcohol (Coalescing Agent): 6.0 kg (0.60%)
  12. In-Can & Dry-Film Biocide (Kathon LX + Carbendazim/IPBC): 4.5 kg (0.45%)
  13. Polyurethane Associative Thickener (Acrysol RM-8W): 2.0 kg (0.20%)
  14. DM Water adjustment: 19.0 kg (1.90%)
- **Manufacturing Procedure**:
  - Charge DM Water into High-Speed Disperser (HSD). Sprinkle HEC under 600 RPM; add Ammonia to hydrate and develop clear gel (10 mins).
  - Add Dispersant, Wetting Agent, and half Antifoam under 800 RPM.
  - Add TiO2 and Calcite 300 mesh. Ramp HSD to 1,400 RPM for 20 minutes until Hegman fineness < 40 microns for the slurry.
  - Reduce speed to 400 RPM. Slowly charge graded Quartz Granules. Mix 10 minutes until uniformly coated.
  - Slowly pump in Pure Acrylic Emulsion, Texanol, and Biocide. Blend at 350 RPM.
  - Adjust final viscosity with RM-8W. Add remaining Antifoam. Discharge into 25kg bags/buckets.
- **Technical Specifications**:
  - Total Solids by Weight: 78.5% ± 1%
  - Specific Gravity: 1.72 ± 0.03 g/cm³
  - Krebs Viscosity (KU): 125–135 KU (Paste consistency for trowel application)
  - Scrub Resistance: > 5,000 cycles (IS 15489)
  - Raw Material Cost (RMC): ₹21.80 / kg (₹545 per 25kg bag) -> Retail MRP: ₹1,150
