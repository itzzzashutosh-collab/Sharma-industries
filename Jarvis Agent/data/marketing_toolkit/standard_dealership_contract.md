## 1. Appointment & Territory Grant
1. Sharma Industries hereby grants the Dealer non-exclusive authorized retail rights to market, stock, and sell Swatch Paints products within the agreed local municipal/pincode territory.
2. The Company agrees to protect the Dealer by refusing direct retail price undercutting within a 2-kilometer radial boundary in urban markets and 5-kilometer boundary in rural mandis.

---

## 2. Pricing & Commercial Terms
1. Products shall be billed at the Company's prevailing Authorized Dealer Wholesale Price List.
2. **Dealer Gross Margin Guarantee**: The Company structures all retail MRPs and dealer wholesale rates such that the minimum retailer gross margin shall not fall below 25% on standard decorative lines and 30% on texture coatings.
3. Prices are inclusive of GST as applicable at the factory gate. Freight is subsidized on full-truckload (FTL) and scheduled route deliveries.

---

## 3. Payment Discipline & Receivables Policy (Fiduciary Safeguard)
1. **Initial Orders**: The first three (3) purchase orders placed by a newly appointed Dealer must be settled on a 100% advance or Cash-on-Delivery (COD) basis.
2. **Standard Credit Term**: Established Tier-1 and Tier-2 dealers are extended a maximum credit period of **seven (7) calendar days** from the invoice dispatch date.
3. **Early Settlement Incentive**: A cash discount of **2.5%** on the basic value shall be credited to the Dealer's ledger for all payments remitted within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery.
4. **Default & Hard Lock Clause**: Any invoice remaining unpaid beyond twenty-one (21) days shall incur interest at 1.5% per month, and the Dealer's account in the Company's CRM (`users.csv`) shall be automatically locked from placing new dispatch orders until full settlement.

---

## 4. Product Quality Assurance & Warranty
1. The Company warrants that all paints, primers, putties, and textures are manufactured in accordance with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS / IS 15489) specifications.
2. In the event of a verified formulation or batch defect (adhesion failure, chalking, in-can gelling), the Company shall replace the defective stock 1:1 at zero cost to the Dealer within 72 hours of inspection by Senior Chemist Shahrukh bhai.
