## Part 1: Official Job Description (For Job Portals, LinkedIn & Distribution)

### About Swatch Paints:
Swatch Paints is a high-performance paint and coatings brand manufactured by Sharma Industries in Rajasthan. We manufacture premium texture coatings (**Swatch Rustic Texture**, **Swatch Roller Coat** in 25kg bags), exterior/interior emulsions (**Swatch Weatherguard**, **Swatch Shine** in 20L, 10L, 4L, 1L), and waterproofing solutions at honest prices with industry-leading dealer margins.

### Territory & Hiring Scope:
We are aggressively hiring Field Sales Executives across **all major districts and trade corridors of Rajasthan (Pan-Rajasthan)**:
- **Central & North Hubs**: Jaipur, Ajmer, Sikar, Alwar, Bikaner.
- **South & West Hubs**: Jodhpur, Udaipur, Bhilwara, Pali, Chittorgarh.
- **Hadoti Hubs**: Kota, Bundi, Baran, Jhalawar.
*(Selected candidates are assigned designated retail beats in their native or preferred district).*

### What We Offer (Compensation & Benefits):
We offer a professional, transparent corporate package combining salary stability with attractive sales incentives:
- **Fixed Monthly Base Salary**: **₹15,000 / month** (Payout on 1st of every month).
- **Fixed Field Travel & Daily Allowance (TA/DA)**: **₹3,000 / month** (₹120/day daily market conveyance & fuel).
- **Monthly Revenue Billing Target**: **₹2,00,000 (₹2.0 Lakhs) / Month** across our complete product catalog:
  - You can sell any mix of products to achieve the target: Texture bags, Weatherguard/Shine emulsions, or Waterproofing!
  - *(Equivalent to approx. 250–290 texture bags or a mixed basket of buckets and bags).*
- **Performance Incentive (Above Target)**:
  - **2.5% direct incentive** on all billing generated above the ₹2,00,000 target.
  - Quarterly consistency bonus of **₹5,000** for achieving 100%+ target for 3 consecutive months.
- **Total Monthly Take-Home Potential**: **₹18,000 to ₹25,000+ / month**.
- **Marketing & Pull Support**: Free 1ft x 1ft physical texture demo boards, product shade cards, dealer display materials, and the high-demand **Painters Growth Tokens (₹50 instant cash token inside every 25kg Rustic bag)** to create automatic painter pull at the counter.

### Product Portfolio & Dealer Trade Prices (For Rep Pitching):
Sales executives sell across the full portfolio at official company trade rates:
1. **Swatch Rustic Texture (25kg Bag)**: Dealer ₹690 | MRP ₹1,150 (Includes **₹50 Painters Growth Token** inside). Bulk rate (≥50 bags): ₹640–₹650.
2. **Swatch Roller Coat (25kg Bag)**: Dealer ₹690 | MRP ₹1,150.
3. **Swatch Weatherguard (Exterior Emulsion)**: 20L Bucket Dealer ₹2,460 | MRP ₹4,100 (Also in 10L, 4L, 1L).
4. **Swatch Shine Emulsion (Interior Emulsion)**: 20L Bucket Dealer ₹2,460 | MRP ₹4,100 (Also in 10L, 4L, 1L).
5. **Swatch Waterproofing Solution**: 5L Jerry Can Dealer ₹1,080 | 1L Bottle Dealer ₹216.
6. **Swatch Top Coat**: 5L Jerry Can Dealer ₹1,500 | 1L Bottle Dealer ₹300.
- **Cash Discount (CD)**: 2% cash discount on invoices cleared within 7 days.

### Key Responsibilities:
1. Conduct 8–12 physical retail counter visits daily across assigned district beats (paint retailers, hardware shops, building material dealers).
2. Pitch the complete Swatch Paints range using 1ft x 1ft sample demo boards and bucket literature.
3. Onboard a minimum of 8–10 new billing counters each month in assigned Rajasthan territory.
4. Achieve the monthly revenue billing target of **₹2,00,000**.
5. Explain the **Painters Growth Tokens** loyalty program (₹50 token inside bag) to local paint contractors and painters.
6. Submit daily route check-ins (9:30 AM) and evening sales summaries (5:30 PM) via WhatsApp to the Hermes AI Sales Operations desk.

### Candidate Profile & Requirements:
- 1 to 4 years of proven field sales experience in Paints, Putty, Cement, Tiles, Adhesives, Hardware, or Building Materials.
- Deep familiarity with dealer trade networks in their local district/market in Rajasthan.
- Must own a two-wheeler with valid driving license and an active smartphone.
- High energy, excellent trade negotiation skills, and uncompromising honesty.
